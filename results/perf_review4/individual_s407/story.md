# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team had a short clock to build a fish from dominoes. Maya asked Priya to pass her the pad, but Priya replied that the fish was already sketched and she was laying the top line from the nose. Maya took the bottom line and assigned the tail lobes: she took the upper one and Leo the lower. Tom waited until Priya's line reached the nose, then ran the bottom line out toward Leo's tail. That hand-off was the turning point. Leo laid the lower lobe and checked the join, while Priya, Maya and Tom kept their hands off. Tom summed it up: "Whole run is connected from the start." When time ran out, all 115 dominoes fell.
