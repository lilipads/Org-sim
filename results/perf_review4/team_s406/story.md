# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Sofia sketched a fish first, with a big body and a round tail. Hana asked for the top line to run from the nose over the back to the tail, with the belly branching off near the nose, so everything would leave the start together. Omar traced the belly while Jack stayed free to patch any gaps.

The turning point came at the tail. Hana warned that its corners were "too sharp for the dominoes," so Sofia redrew it as one wide, rounded sweep. Sofia asked Hana to watch where it curled back toward the belly, and the join was closed side-on rather than head-on. Omar cleared the strays under the top line, and the team took their hands off the table.

When the start was pushed, the whole chain fell: 110 of 110. Blind judges guessed everything from a tadpole to a balloon, with fish among the guesses, but the team had built the fish they'd planned.
