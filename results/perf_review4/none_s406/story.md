## In short

At one table, four teammates set out to build a fish from dominoes before time ran out. Hana sketched the plan: "Let's do a fish: big body, forked tail." She laid the top line from the start while Sofia took the bottom, and Omar and Jack waited near the nose to help.

The first tangle came when Jack offered to take the top line. Hana asked him to hold off so they wouldn't double up. Once her batch landed, she sent him on toward the nose, but his stroke stopped mid-body. Omar picked it up and carried it the rest of the way.

With time running low, the bottom line still fell short of the nose, and the team was nearly out of dominoes. Sofia lifted the loose pale pieces by the tail to get some back and carried the line to the tip. Omar spotted a small gap at the nose and nudged it closed. Then everyone went hands off. "Let the fish swim!" 244 of 249 fell.
