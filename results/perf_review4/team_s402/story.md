## In short

Sofia had a plan: a big fish facing left, with a back, a belly, and two tails. Hana took the top tail, Omar the belly, Jack the bottom tail, and Sofia the back. But where the back met the tail, the turn was too sharp, and the fall stalled. Hana was ready on the top tail, waiting.

Sofia tried to fix the notch by bridging, lifting, and sweeping the back out wider. Meanwhile, Omar's belly reached the lower notch, and Jack's bottom tail went dark. Omar bridged the gap at the right edge, but the top tail stayed pale. Hana called for hands off.

Sofia admitted her fixes hadn't caught. "The fish falls nearly everything and reads clearly," she said. Hana agreed the body was dark and the fish read clearly. Then came the push, and 105 of 120 dominoes fell. Every team member called it a fish, and every blind judge agreed it was one too.
