# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

## In short

Four teammates had twenty minutes to draw a fish out of dominoes. Leo set the plan: "Let's do a fish, big and simple, facing right." Priya laid the body first, curving from the tail round to the nose. Maya took the bottom line, and Leo drew the tail and fin. Maya took the lower tail lobe while Leo finished the upper one. Tracing carried on from where it stopped, so nothing got redone.

The fin was the trouble. It showed pale, so it wasn't catching the top line. Tom checked the joint, and Maya pinpointed the break: "One domino is jammed too close to the next one." Tom lifted it out and bridged the gap with a connect. Then he reported, "Fin's catching now, it shows dark all the way out to the tip."

A few pale strays stood under the fin. Tom left them alone rather than risk the fin, and Priya ran the final check while everyone kept their hands off. At the push, 122 of 125 dominoes fell, and all three blind judges guessed fish.
