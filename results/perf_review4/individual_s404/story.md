# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Four teammates had twenty minutes to build a domino picture, and they chose a fish. Jack proposed it, and Hana sketched it on the pad: one line over the back to a flared tail, and one round the belly, both leaving from the nose. Hana's back line ran from the tail to the nose, and Sofia waited to start the belly until it arrived.

The first turning point came when Jack offered to carry the back line on to the nose. Hana had already finished that stretch, so he stayed back. The second came when Hana split the long belly. Sofia stopped at the low point, and Hana laid the rest to the lower tail while Omar checked the middle. Sofia then spotted a doubled bit on the back. Jack explained it was meant to be a dorsal fin, and since it stayed connected, it was left alone.

When the push came, all 125 of 125 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a fish, and the blind judges agreed.
