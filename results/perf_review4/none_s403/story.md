## In short

Maya sketched a big, simple fish on the pad and took the top curve herself. Tom took the bottom curve, and Priya laid the lower tail lobe. Leo took the upper lobe.

When Maya came across, Tom said the whole fish lit up from the nose. Then Leo caught a problem: "the upper tail lobe still looks pale to me, so it isn't connected yet." Tom agreed it was his mistake. Leo's nudge placed nothing, and his bridge didn't take, so he rebuilt the lobe from scratch while everyone kept their hands off. "Upper lobe's down and looks dark to me," he reported. The others agreed, and the fish sat untouched until the push.

When the start domino fell, 99 of 101 went down. Blind judges guessed fish, leaf or lens shapes with forked tails.
