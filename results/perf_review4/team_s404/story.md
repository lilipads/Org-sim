## In short

Jack grabbed the pad and sketched a big fish, side view, nose on the left, drawn as one run that split around the body. Sofia took the belly line, and Omar and Hana took the tail. Then Hana spotted a flaw: starting at the tail would force sharp turns the fall couldn't make. The team moved the start to the nose, and Jack redrew the fish from there.

Near the end, with the clock nearly up, the nose still looked pale. Jack cleared the tangle there and rebuilt from the start, while Sofia kept her hands off. Hana bridged the top line, and Omar warned Sofia that one tight domino at the end of the start row was stalling the chain. Sofia re-seated it and joined the row into the belly.

Everyone agreed it was a fish. The blind judges guessed a bird, a pair of tweezers, and a stray scribble. When the start was pushed, only 13 of 122 fell.
