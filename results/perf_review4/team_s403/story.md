## In short

Priya wanted a big fish with a smooth body and no sharp corners. Maya sketched it on the pad, then took the bottom line while Priya traced the top line and the start domino. Leo checked the nose and reported, "Nose looks good, everything's connected from the start," but warned that "the long lines are going to be our bottleneck." He carried the top line toward the tail fork, and Tom took the lower lobe where Maya's line stopped.

The turning point came when a few pale dominoes sat unconnected on the top line. Priya cleared the stray pale ones by the tail fork. Maya stood the middle ones back up, then pulled the extras out, leaving one stray she judged harmless. Hands came off the lines, and the team waited for the push.

When the start domino fell, 107 of 108 dominoes went down. Everyone said fish, and the blind judges agreed on "fish outline facing left" and "a fish with a forked tail."
