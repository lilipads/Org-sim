# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Hana chose a fish and split the work: Sofia on the bottom curve, Omar on the upper tail, Jack on the lower tail. Hana laid the body from the nose. A gap at the top of the body was closed, and Sofia reported, "Top of the body is connected now."

The crossing where the tails met was the trouble spot. Hana's bridge "won't take," so she relaid Jack's lower tail from scratch while everyone else kept their hands off. Then Sofia offered to fix the notch from the top, and Hana said yes. The two worked apart so they wouldn't bump each other.

The bottom-right corner of the loop never caught. Hana's last bridge went in, but the chain still stopped there. With the body and both tails lit, the team went hands off. When time ran out, 130 of 145 fell.
