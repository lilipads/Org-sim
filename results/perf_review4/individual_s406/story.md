# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Sofia sketched a big fish facing left, with its start at the nose. She took the top line, Omar took the bottom, and the tail was left for Hana and Jack. Omar was already well into his bottom line when he asked Hana to take its end past the crossing, so they could meet in the middle. Jack spotted the top line stopping short of the tail and carried it out to the tip. Hana joined the bottom line through the crossing while Sofia finished the top tail. Jack noticed a doubled-up joint near the crossing, and Omar offered to nudge a domino there, then decided it looked fine. Sofia inspected the tail, left the crossing alone, and called for hands off. Everyone agreed. When the facilitator pushed the start, 121 of 124 dominoes fell, and every player called it a fish.
