# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Four teammates chose a fish and set to work with dominoes on the pad. Leo laid the top line from the nose, Maya took the belly, and Priya took the tail. Tom offered to help, but Leo asked him to hold off so nobody doubled up, and Tom turned to checking joints instead.

The nose was the first tricky spot. Leo rounded the tight turn, then checked it: "I'm checking that joint now, since everything below depends on it." Later, Priya's tail stroke ran up to meet the top line, and Leo bridged the upper tail, nudging stray dominoes into line. Maya kept watch for pale spots until the whole fish looked dark from the start.

When time ran out, the facilitator pushed the start, and the team called out, "Fingers crossed, go fish!" 123 of 125 dominoes fell. Blind judges saw a fish, though some also saw a leaf or a bird.
