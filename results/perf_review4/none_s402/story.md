# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team chose a fish: a big, simple shape with a forked tail. Hana sketched it and started the top of the body at the nose, while Sofia traced the bottom line. Then Sofia spotted a pale stretch after the first bend in the top run. Hana closed that joint, and Sofia confirmed the body was dark all the way through, so it would fall from the start.

Omar took the upper tail and Jack the lower. Hana reminded them to keep the tips "curved and soft, because sharp corners will stop the fall." Sofia told Omar there was "no need to join up at the notch," so he let the tip end near the other line. Jack's lower tail was soon dark and ready.

Hana checked the notch and noticed a tight domino near the fork. It wasn't stopping anything, so nobody touched it. Hands came off, and everyone waited for the push.

When Omar shouted "timber!", all 118 dominoes fell. Everyone had called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed a fish outline too.
