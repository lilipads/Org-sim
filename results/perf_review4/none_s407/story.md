# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya opened with a plan for a big fish, laid from the nose along a top line and a bottom line, ending in a forked tail. Leo put the sketch up, and the team divided the lines. Tom took the bottom, and Priya moved in to help with the slow top line. The turning point came when Priya offered to carry the top line from the hump toward the tail while Maya jumped ahead to the middle of the back. Tom carried the back line into the upper fork, and Leo checked the joins, noticing that the back "looks doubled up in a couple of spots." Tom then reviewed the whole run and found nothing loose. The team agreed on "hands off," and when the push came, every domino fell: 131 of 131.
