# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish from dominoes against the clock: a nose on the left, a forked tail on the right. Priya planned the top line, Maya the bottom, and Tom and Leo the tail. The first turning point came when Leo spotted that the start sat in the tail notch, so the fall would miss the body. Maya moved the start to the nose and redrew the sketch. Priya noticed the pad still showed the old version, so Maya drew it again. Tom worried the lines were too long to reach the tail in time, but Maya declined his extra strokes. She let him take the far end of the bottom run instead. Maya and Tom carried the lines toward the crossing while Leo nudged a stray domino back into line. Leo checked the joints, and then the team agreed on one thing: "Hands off and let it run." The whole fish fell, 111 of 111.
