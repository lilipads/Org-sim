## In short

Four teammates set out to draw a fish with dominoes. Priya sketched the idea on the pad, a fish facing left with a swooshy tail, and laid the top line. Maya took the bottom line, Tom carried the top round the curve toward the nose, and Leo watched the joins.

The first big turn came at the nose. Leo found a jammed domino that stopped the fall, fixed it, and called out, "Nose is fixed, everything standing now falls from the start." Priya then started the bottom line from the tail while Maya worked the belly. Tom found a domino sitting off to the side in the belly seam and closed it.

Near the end, Priya spotted a tight domino in the tail. Tom nudged it, closed the last gap at the tip, and Priya called it: "Hands off, everyone. Let it run!"

Result: 116 of 119 fell. The blind judges all saw a fish.
