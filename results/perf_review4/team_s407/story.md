## In short

Maya sketched the plan on the pad: "Let's do a fish!" A big body, lines splitting at the nose, and a rounded tail on the right. Tom took the top line, Priya the bottom, and Leo the tail.

The first snag came when Leo flagged a sharp pinch where the top line met the tail. Maya tested the fall, found it ran through, and told him to take that corner smooth instead of changing the sketch.

Tom finished the top line, and the whole run fell from the nose. Next came the bottom line. Priya carried it round the lower tail while Leo came down the right side, and they met near the bottom corner. Priya closed the last joint: "The run looks connected all the way round."

Maya called for hands off, and the team kept clear until the push. In the end, 126 of 126 pieces fell. Most blind judges guessed a fish, though a few saw a leaf.
