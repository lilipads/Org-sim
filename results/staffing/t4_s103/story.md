# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

**The Fish Nobody Could Read**

Maya, Tom, Priya and Leo had one timed round to build a fish out of dominoes, and Maya set the plan. She sketched it on the pad and began at the tail tip, while Leo took the belly and Priya the lower fin. Early on, the plan had the fin closing back onto the tip. Maya stopped that: "Keep the fork open at the body joint. That split is what reads as a fish tail. A closed loop looks like a blob." The team dropped the closing line.

Tom checked each joint as it landed. Once the belly was down, he said, "Fork looks good from here." When Priya's fin went on, he confirmed the chain ran "from the start right through to the fin tip, nothing broken." Maya was still working on her top line, with the nose gap yet to fix, as the clock wound down.

Result: 64 of 72 fell. Everyone called it a fish. The blind judges saw eggplants, balloons and leaves.
