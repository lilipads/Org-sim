## In short

At a table with a pad of paper and a set of dominoes, Hana chose to sketch a fish from the side. She started at the tail tip and laid the top edge, then asked for help: "Could someone take the bottom edge... from that same tail tip?" The log shows no one answering, so she kept building alone. The top edge stopped short of the nose, and she extended it. The turning point came when she noticed the bottom edge looked pale at the tail and wrote, "Bottom edge isn't joined to the start yet." She bridged it back to the start, closed the gap at the nose, and confirmed the whole outline connected. With time nearly up, she asked everyone to leave the top edge alone while she carried the bottom edge out to the nose. When the start domino was pushed, all of it fell: 99 of 99. The table saw a fish. The blind judges saw a lens-shaped eye.
