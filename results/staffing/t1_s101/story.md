## In short

Maya chose a fish: a simple outline with a big tail fin, so it would read clearly. With time running short, she took the pad and laid the first stroke from the tail tip herself. "I'm placing the start," she said. She sketched the tail on the left and the eye up top, then bridged the snout and the belly.

The turning point came late. The fin was still open and had to close back to the start, but nobody had taken it. Maya asked whoever was free to trace the start, and when no one answered, she laid it herself. Then she closed the last fin gap, asking everyone not to push until she said so.

When the start was pushed, 37 of 63 fell. Blind judges guessed a winding river or road, not a fish.
