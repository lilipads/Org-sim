# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya chose a fish, since its forked tail would read from the outline alone. Leo traced the top edge from the tail, Priya took the belly line from the nose back to the notch, and Maya laid the fins as branches off the body, away from the start spot. Tom held the pad, ready to fix anything.

The first real snag came at the notch, where the lower tail didn't quite meet. Priya nudged a sideways domino into line. Then Leo found one jammed at the nose and re-nudged it, and Priya held her check until he said it was in.

As the clock wound down, Priya wasn't sure her notch nudge had gone in. Tom checked it and found that "the fall runs from the start straight through it." Leo made a final inspection from the start, and the team kept their hands off. When the facilitator pushed the start domino, the fish fell. All 84 dominoes went down.
