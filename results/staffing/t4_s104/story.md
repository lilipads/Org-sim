## In short

**One Fish, Many Hands**

The team had a short clock and one shared plan: a fish. Sofia sketched it and wanted to start at the tail, but Hana spotted the flaw. Fins leaving the start in opposite directions wouldn't hold together. Sofia agreed, "START at the nose works better for the fins," and the body began flowing rightward from there.

Each person took a stroke. Sofia laid the top, Omar the belly, Jack the top fin, and Hana the bottom fin. A bump knocked over a pair of dominoes near the nose, and Sofia stood them back up while she kept tracing. Hana cleared a jammed piece at the tip, then checked the gaps. Omar confirmed the belly end was clean. Near the end, Hana found the belly pale at the nose and closed that gap.

When the top reached the tail, Hana went ahead with the join. Omar's last catch was Jack's fin, still pale by the middle, and Jack was closing it as the log ends.

Result: 81 of 159 fell. Every builder said fish. The blind judges saw a bird, a river, and a tree branch.
