## In short

The team set out to build a fish against the clock. Priya sketched it on the pad: "I'll take the top line from the nose." Maya took the bottom line, but it kept curving up toward the top line, and Leo spotted the problem. Priya stepped in: "I'll lay the bottom line myself so you don't have to redo it."

The tail was crowded. Maya cleared a stray drop, and Tom worked on a jammed low arm. Leo lifted a batch of dominoes below the junction by mistake and owned it: "Tom, that was me, sorry." Tom laid the upright at the back of the tail, and Maya trimmed an overshoot. Near the end, Priya's join ran off the edge of the table, so she tried a direct connect. Leo nudged a kink, and Maya confirmed the tail was live. When the start was pushed, 120 of 168 fell.
