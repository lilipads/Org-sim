## In short

A team set out to build a fish in a timed round. Leo suggested it, and Priya split the strokes: Maya took the bottom body, Tom the upper tail, Leo the lower tail, and Priya the nose and top line. Tom laid the start, and the body and upper tail soon went up.

The trouble came when Leo clipped the junction and knocked dominoes flat. Priya's top body stayed pale, and the lower tail refused to fall. Maya traced the problem: "The corner at the bottom of the tail edge turns too sharply." Nudging failed, so Maya relaid the whole lower tail, and Leo later closed the left side.

Late on, Maya changed course: "I'm branching a straight run down from the start line to the top of the tail edge." The run landed all the way down, and Tom joined its end into the pale lower tail.

At the push, the fish body and upper tail fell, while the lower tail stayed pale longest. The judges read it differently: all three guessed fish, but each also named a leaf or a boat. 117 of 218 dominoes fell.
