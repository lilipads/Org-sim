## In short

Leo suggested a fish, Priya sketched it, and the team split the strokes. Maya took the bottom body, Tom the upper tail, and Leo the lower tail. Maya kept trying to bridge the pale top body line while Leo and Tom stayed off her corner. Priya cleared a stray line below the tail, Leo pulled up more dominoes for spares, and Tom patched the nose. By the end, Maya was out of dominoes, and her last bridge ran toward the upper right. "That one's on me," she admitted. Leo's final join placed nothing, and the team ran out. "Hands off everyone, let it fall," Maya said. Only 139 of 250 fell. Everyone said fish, but the blind judges guessed rope, a river, and a plant stem. Nice work, team.
