## In short

Leo proposed a fish, and Maya sketched it on the pad: lead-in, top line, bottom line, tail. Tom joined the bottom line into the tail, and Leo ran the top line toward the back. Priya took the joints where the lead-in met the lines, but her join drifted right instead of climbing into the top line. Leo cleared the strays, and Priya stepped back. Tom took over the front joint, but it wasn't catching. His join stayed pale, so he tried again from the dark run. With time nearly gone, Maya laid a backup join further along, and Tom stopped clearing so he wouldn't pull it up. Leo checked the middle of the top line and found no breaks, so the front joint was the only thing holding it back. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed branches, rivers, and vines. In the end, 112 of 250 dominoes fell.
