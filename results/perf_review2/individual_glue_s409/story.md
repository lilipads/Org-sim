## In short

The team set out to build a fish from dominoes against the clock. Maya took the pad and sketched the body, then split the work: Leo took the lead-in and top body, Tom and Priya the bottom and tail, and Maya the joins. Early on, the tail went down and Leo claimed the tail junction. Then Priya spotted a break at the nose: "the lead-in runs past where the body lines start." She bridged it and relaid the bottom line. As time ran short, the back edge stayed pale, and Tom and Priya found themselves on the same spot. Maya pulled loose dominoes back into the shared pile so others could use them. Leo bridged a jammed gap, and Maya fixed just below it. Then everyone went hands off. In the end, 91 of 246 dominoes fell, and the judges saw a tree branch or a river, not a fish.
