# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team agreed on a fish facing left. Leo sketched it on the pad and split the strokes: he took the top line, Maya the bottom, Tom the tail, and Priya the joins. Then the tail came up as a rounded loop, not the pointed fin on the pad. Leo laid the fin himself, but his first attempt ended up inside the loop. Maya raised an alarm about a break on the fin, then admitted, "Sorry for the false alarm." Tom took down a stray vertical line, and Priya cleared the leftover loop in the body. The real turning point came when Leo bridged a gap in the bottom line just before the tail. Priya closed the last break at the tail, and Maya ran a final check from the start. Then everyone took their hands off. The result: 109 of 120 fell, and everyone said fish. Blind judges saw a fish, a bird, and a leaf, with one guessing a whale.
