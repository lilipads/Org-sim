# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Priya suggested a fish, and Maya sketched it on the pad. Priya took the top line, Leo the bottom, and Maya the tail fork. Everyone watched the join at the nose. Then Tom stepped in, curving a run of dominoes round the nose into Leo's line, so Maya could leave that gap. Maya checked the whole run end to end, and Leo looked closely at the nose. A few stray dominoes sat beside the outline, but the fall carried from the start round the whole fish, both tail tips included. Then everyone kept their hands off. When the timer ran out, 117 of 119 dominoes fell. All four teammates called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed a fish, a dolphin, a bird, a tadpole, and a boat hull.
