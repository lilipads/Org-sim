## In short

Priya sketched a left-facing fish and split the work: she took the top line, Maya the bottom, and Tom and Leo the two tail pieces. Tom noticed the start only reached a few dominoes. "Join didn't take at the nose," he reported, and after a second nudge he bridged it properly. Leo laid the back half of the body, and his bump knocked a few dominoes over. Maya asked him to stand them back up. Tom laid the crossing join, then found the upper tail jammed and relaid it. The last trouble was the notch at the fork. Priya spotted two open ends there and left the join to Maya, who needed spares, so Priya returned hers to the pile. Priya cleared a stray piece under the body to free more dominoes, and Leo fixed the jammed tail. With Maya's last join going in, the team called it a fish, and 199 of 248 fell.
