# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

On a busy table, Priya sketched a big fish in seconds: a fat oval body, a forked tail on the right, and a start at the nose. Maya took the bottom body line, Tom the top, and Leo the tail pieces. Then the roles tangled. Priya kept reassigning lines until Tom, already a third of the way along his, held his ground. "Tom, stay on the top body line," Priya finally wrote.

The real turning point came at the top tail, which stayed pale. Priya's connect could not make the turn, so she handed it over: "Maya, yes please, redo the top tail from the pad." Maya redid it while Leo dropped his connect and Tom kept his hands off.

With five minutes left, Maya called it: "Top tail's connected." Priya checked the run from the nose and found it dark "all the way through to the tail tip." Then everyone sat on their hands. In the end, 121 of 129 dominoes fell. Every blind judge guessed at a fish, among other shapes.
