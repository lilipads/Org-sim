# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The table had a fish to build and a clock running down. Leo proposed the fish, and Maya sketched it and split the work: Tom took the top line, Priya the bottom, and Leo the tail. Maya floated between them, taking the bottom join.

Priya spotted a pale loop hanging below the tail that wasn't in the sketch, and Leo cleared the loose part.

Tom found the real break at the bottom corner of the tail. He bridged it rather than redoing Leo's stroke, and Priya urged him to make it "a bit rounder rather than a hard corner."

Then Maya reported, "I can't actually see a jammed one at the top of the tail." Leo admitted the spot he'd flagged was by the pale strays, not the live line. The team agreed: "Hands off from here, everyone."

The fish fell 137 of 149 dominoes. Every player had called it a fish, but the judges guessed fish, leaf, bird, or boat.
