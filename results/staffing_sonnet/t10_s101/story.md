## In short

At the start, Hana sketched a fish facing left and split the work: Maya traced the top, Tom the belly, Priya the upper fin, and Leo the lower fin. By the halfway point, Sofia could report that "everything's connected so far," and Grace patched a sharp turn at the tail joint. Sofia backed off the lower fin when Leo said it was queued, and Omar agreed to cover gaps.

Then the lower fin ran late. With little time left, Jack spotted a wide gap on the left side of the notch, and Leo decided to re-lay the lower fin rather than patch it. Grace offered to take the far half, but Leo asked her to hold off. Sam then carried the far stretch up to the notch, and Omar told Grace to stay off it so nothing got lifted. Sam declared, "Tail's joined," and the team kept their hands off until the push. In the end, 130 of 131 dominoes fell, and everyone called it a fish.
