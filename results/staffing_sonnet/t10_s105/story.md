## In short

Sofia pitched a big fish facing left, and Leo put her sketch onto the pad. Lines were claimed one by one: Leo took the nose and top curve, Hana and Maya took the bottom curve, and Tom took the tail. Grace's drifting run was pulled out after Hana and Sam flagged it, and Hana bridged the bottom curve so the body carried through to the tail point.

The tail then stalled. Tom's domino snagged on a sharp corner at the join, and he asked Sofia and Omar to hold off: "I don't want us bumping each other." Hana said the tail read more like a hook than a triangle, so Sofia laid the upper edge up to the top of the upright. Priya connected the upper edge, Sam doubled up at the same joint, and Tom's bridge went into the pale lower edge.

With time nearly gone, Grace said, "no new pieces now," and everyone kept their hands off. When it fell, 208 of 222 dominoes went down, and the blind judges saw a fish with a tangled tail.
