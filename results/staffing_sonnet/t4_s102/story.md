# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Jack opened with a plan: "How about a fish?" He took the lead-in and the start, while Sofia drew the nose and the top and bottom lines. Hana spotted a problem. The triangle tail had sharp corners that a fall would not turn, so she redrew it as two open curves off the back joint and asked everyone to skip the old triangle.

Omar bridged the lower tail, which still looked pale. Sofia checked the back joint, found a sharp turn where the body lines met, and offered to patch it from the body side. Jack took down a vertical line that looked like the old back edge. Omar cleared a stray line rising from the bottom of the table. Hana left the remaining pale curves alone, since they were not connected and clearing them risked the tail.

Then the team went hands off. Sofia cheered, "Great fish, everyone!" and Omar joked about sneezing as the countdown ran. When the start domino was pushed, the fish fell. Blind judges guessed a fish, though some saw a leaf. In the end, 112 of 160 dominoes fell.
