## In short

**The Fish That Almost Got Away**

Hana grabbed the pad and sketched a fish, handing out strokes: Maya and Tom on the body, Priya and Leo on the tail lines. Jack asked for a line to close the open tail, and Hana put it on the pad as stroke six for him to build. Then trouble gathered at the split. Sam was jammed there, and a pale line dropped down from it. Hana and Grace both lifted it, and Hana asked Sam to help at the tail instead, where "we're thin." Omar stepped in to start the bottom tail line.

Jack's closing line drifted up and to the right. Maya, Priya, and Grace all spotted it. Sofia took the top end and ran it straight down, while Priya lifted a curl from her middle stretch. Sam closed a small offset in the middle of the fin, and Sofia nudged in a wobble near the top tail.

"Hands off, everybody," Priya said. When the start domino was pushed, 129 of 132 fell.
