## In short

Hana set the plan: "let's do a fish, nose on the left, tail fin on the right." Sofia laid the start and lead-in, and the team split the lines. Hana took the top of the body, Maya the bottom, Priya the upper tail, and Leo the lower tail. Jack floated, ready to patch breaks, and Sam offered to help. Priya replied, "I've got the upper tail," and Sam stepped back.

Once the lines met at the tail, the body held. The fin was harder. Sofia closed the back edge, then found the lower tip loose. Maya inspected the chain and named the rough spots, and Sofia worked to bridge them while everyone else kept clear of the tail.

With the timer nearly out, Sofia called it: the lower tip needed a full redo, and there wasn't time. She left it alone. The team's final result was 147 of 165 dominoes falling. Every player called the picture a fish, though the blind judges saw a looped bird, a leaf on a stem, and a fish with a looped tail.
