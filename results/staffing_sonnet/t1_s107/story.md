# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 1

*1 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo chose a fish facing left, sketched it, and started laying dominoes. Early on, the bottom of the fish didn't show up, so he laid it again before adding the tail. As the facilitator warned that only what was connected to the start domino would fall, Leo kept building: "Bottom's down. Now the lower tail and the back edge of the tail." Soon the fish was all down and connected. Near the end, he checked the tail joint, saw that "everything still connects," and left it alone so nothing would get bumped. When the start domino was pushed, all 124 fell. The blind judges guessed everything from a bird to a leaf, but they agreed on one thing: Leo's picture was a fish.
