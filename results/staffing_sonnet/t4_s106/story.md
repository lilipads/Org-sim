## In short

At a table of four, Hana sketched a fish facing left and split the work. Sofia took the top of the body, Omar the bottom, and Jack the tail. The body lines reached the tail joint, which grew crowded.

Omar spotted a little hook past the joint that looked odd. Hana re-laid the lower lobe so it would fork cleanly and asked everyone to keep their hands clear. Jack stepped off to help Omar with the upper lobe, and Sofia checked that the whole chain still carried from the start.

With minutes running low, the lower lobe fell but stayed pale. Sofia diagnosed a jammed domino right at the joint: "Try lifting that one." Hana did, re-laid the lobe, and it came down pale again. Sofia stood ready to connect from the top line's end.

Everyone agreed it was a fish, though the blind judges split between a fish outline, a leaf, and a loop. In the end, 93 of 107 dominoes fell.
