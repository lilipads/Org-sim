## In short

The team had a tight time limit to build a fish out of dominoes. Hana sketched it first, a side-on fish with a big forked tail, and split up the work: Jack took the top body line, Omar the bottom, and Sofia the tail fork.

Jack's top line reached the tail first, and Hana checked the split joint: "everything standing falls from the start." Omar's bottom line lagged a bit behind. When the tail proved to be a lot for a pair of hands, Hana said, "I'll build the lower fork," and took that section on.

The turning point came near the end. Hana spotted a stray hook by the bottom tip and wrote, "If it isn't meant to be there, pull it." Sofia confirmed it was hers and pulled it away. Then everyone kept their hands off and waited for the push.

The blind judges guessed a left-facing fish, a loopy doodle, and a cartoon sea creature. Every builder called it a fish. Result: 118 of 118 fell.
