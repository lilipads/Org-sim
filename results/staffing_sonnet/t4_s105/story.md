# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Leo proposed a fish, side view, with a forked tail, and the team split the work. Maya drew the top line from the nose, Tom took the belly, Priya the upper tail, and Leo the lower one. Tom kept his belly lined up with Maya's split, and when he reported "Belly's done, everyone," Maya kept going on the top line and checked where the body met the tail.

The turning point came at the notch between the tail tips. Tom suggested bringing Leo's back edge in toward it, but Leo said the edge already curled in, so he left it alone. Maya called the notch "just where the two lines end," and Priya and Tom agreed. The team kept their hands off, and when the facilitator pushed the start domino, the fish fell. All 122 dominoes went down.
