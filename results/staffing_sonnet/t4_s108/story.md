# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team chose a fish. Jack sketched it, with the nose as the start and the line splitting over the top and under the belly. Sofia, Omar, and Hana each took a part, but their lanes kept colliding. Omar held the belly, and Sofia pulled out a spur that was in the way. Hana claimed the tail, but as the clock ran down, the fin stalled. Jack laid the tail himself, then split the stubborn fin into simpler lines. Hana nudged a jammed domino at the crossing, while Sofia and Omar took the diagonal and back edge. The top corner still wouldn't turn, so Jack added a curved hook and kept bridging it, asking everyone to keep their hands off. When the start was pushed, 119 of 141 fell. Everyone called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed fish too, some spotting an ichthys-style symbol.
