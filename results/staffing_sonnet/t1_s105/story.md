# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 1

*1 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.

## In short

Tom started alone. "Nobody else here, so I'll just sketch a fish and get building." He worked from the bottom up, and reported, "Bottom half's done," before moving on to the top of the fish and the tail fin. Each warning from the facilitator repeated the rule: only what connected to the start domino would fall.

The turning point came near the end. With two minutes left, Tom was "just finishing the tail," and he still had no start domino down. Only then did he lay it, "running the body from it." When the facilitator pushed the start, 11 of 39 dominoes fell. People said it was a fish, but the blind judges guessed a thin stick, a crack, or a dark diagonal line.
