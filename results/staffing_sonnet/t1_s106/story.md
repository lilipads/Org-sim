# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 1

*1 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.

## In short

Omar had the table to himself, so he chose a fish. "Easy outline, and the tail says it all," he wrote at the start. He sketched the body first, then the tail, and planned to check for gaps. As the clock ran down, he kept the pace up: "Body's nearly there, tail goes up next."

The facilitator called out each warning. When time ran out, the start domino was pushed, and only the dominoes connected to it fell. 39 of 40 went down.

The reveal split the room. People said the picture was a fish. The blind judges saw something else: a domino chain curving over, dominoes toppling in an arc, a flat row leading into a curve. Their guesses were waves, ramps and hooks, not a fish at all.

Omar's solo build shows how a clear plan can look different once it is tipped. The fish was clear to its maker, but the judges only saw curves.
