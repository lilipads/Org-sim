Omar started it at the junction, but Hana spotted pale dominoes that weren't connected where the line turned too sharply. Sam's lower fin line curved back toward the body, and Grace lifted the stray bit. Then Tom moved pieces he thought were stray, only to learn they formed Omar's back edge. "Sorry Omar, that's on me," he said. With minutes running out, Maya cleared the jammed upper arm, Grace and Leo nudged the junction, and Omar lined up the back edge.
