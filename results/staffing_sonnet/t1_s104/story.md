## In short

When time was up, the start domino was pushed. Nothing fell. The final result was zero of 16.

Hana said the picture was a fish. The blind judges saw something else. One guessed a "blank off-white canvas with no visible content." Another guessed an "empty placeholder image." A third guessed "faint, very low-contrast content too subtle to see."

The turning point was simple: nothing was connected to the start domino. The fish that Hana had in mind never reached the judges, who saw only an almost empty canvas.
