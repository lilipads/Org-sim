# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya chose a fish: "big body and a clear triangle tail." She took the top line, Tom took the bottom, and Priya took the tail, with Leo ready to check the joints. The body lines ran slow, and Leo's tail waited until Tom finished the bottom line and Priya laid the tail herself. Tracing kept failing, so Priya set the diagonals by hand while Maya sketched the closing edge for Leo to take.

That edge came out pale, unhooked from the top corner. Leo tried to bridge the gap, but the corner sat against the table edge, and the edge couldn't hook on without a sharp turn. He dropped it: "The open tail still reads as a fish." Everyone kept their hands off, the body stayed connected from the start, and the team called out, "Go fish go!" The little fish ran, and 104 of 113 fell.
