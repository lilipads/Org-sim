## In short

**The Fish That Fell**

Leo started it with a simple idea: "How about a fish?" Sofia sketched the outline on the pad, and the team split it into lines. Hana took the top, Maya the bottom, and Sofia the forked tail. Omar kept off the tail and watched the joint where the body lines met, and Tom nudged a jammed domino there into line. Grace closed a small gap just before the tail.

The tail wasn't catching. Maya found pale pieces cut off from the start, and Tom traced it again from the pad. Time ran short. Sam had already run the top arm, so Hana suggested splitting the rest, and Tom took the lower arm and the back edge. Leo spotted a possible gap where the arms met, but Sam and Priya checked and found that the chain traced through. The team went hands off, and when time ran out, the whole fish fell.
