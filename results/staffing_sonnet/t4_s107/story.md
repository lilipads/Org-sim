# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

A small team had a short timer to draw an animal in dominoes, and they chose a fish. Priya sketched the outline and took the tail, Maya drew the top body line, and Tom took the bottom. Leo floated, ready to check the joints once the lines reached the tail.

When Tom noticed the lower tip hooking back toward the body, he asked, "Priya, do you want to redo that last stretch pointing out instead?" Priya lifted the little hook so the tip pointed outward, then checked that the chain still ran all the way into it. Leo's line met hers at the middle of the fork, closing the loop.

With the clock nearly out, everyone kept their hands off. "The whole fish is still connected from the nose," Priya confirmed. Joking about the risk, she said the worst case was "a very committed sardine."

When the start domino was pushed, all 120 fell. Each teammate called it a fish, and the blind judges agreed.
