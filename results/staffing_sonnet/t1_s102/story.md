# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 1

*1 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.

## In short

Sofia took the solo table and chose a fish: a round body and a big forked tail. She sketched it, built the top line, and moved on to the belly and tails. The lower tail fork gave her trouble. It didn't land, so she laid it again. Later the upper tail went up, but the lower fork failed a second time. As the clock ran down, the facilitator warned that only what connected to the start domino would fall. Sofia checked the chain and found the tails and belly still missing. She laid them down, tails first, then the belly. When the start domino went, all 60 pieces fell. Blind judges guessed a fish with a forked tail, among other things, and the people who watched said it was a fish.
