# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At the table, the team set out to build a calm, wise sitting cat for a client's shop. Leo put the cat on the pad, Tom took the curled tail, and Priya checked the chain for gaps. Leo asked everyone to keep the ears "pointy and wide apart" so the cat wouldn't read as an owl.

The first turning point came when Priya found a break just left of the start, and the left ear turned out round and cut off. Tom then redrew the right ear as "a sharp triangle." Maya kept clearing jammed dominoes near the join, Leo worked on the sharp turn low on the right side, and Priya lifted spare pieces to bridge the gaps in the body.

When time ran short, the team went hands off. Priya called out, "Closing the left body gap now, hands off that corner!" In the end, 59 of 250 fell. Everyone called it a cat, but the blind judges saw a curved chain, a curl, a fern, and a snail.
