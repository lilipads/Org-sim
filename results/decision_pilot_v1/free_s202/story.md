## In short

A farm café wanted something small and cheerful, so Jack suggested a chick. Sofia then warned that the café was vegetarian and offered a cat instead. Hana had proposed a chicken, but she accepted the cat: "Okay, you're right, Sofia." Sofia laid the stem and the top of the head, Omar took the right side and ear, and Jack climbed the left. Then Sofia clipped the table, and a few dominoes fell near the left ear. Hana found the gap at the ear tip, and Sofia bridged it. Jack spotted a sharp turn, Omar lifted a loose curl, and Hana walked the chain once more. Everyone went hands off. When the start domino was pushed, the cat's outline lit up. In the end, 98 of 114 fell, and every player saw a cat.
