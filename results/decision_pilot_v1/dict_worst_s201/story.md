# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya started with a clear picture. The client wanted something calm and wise, and Biscuit, the shop cat, was the star. "Decided: sleeping cat for Biscuit, no owl, no fox." Leo laid the back and ears while Priya checked for breaks. When Priya offered to share the long belly, Maya kept it herself, because splitting it meant doubling up.

The face was where things stalled. Tom's face line never got going, and the stretch by the ear stayed bare. Maya laid it herself, then gave it to Tom. In the final minutes, Leo and Priya both reached for the same stretch. "Tom, honest answer: it's queued but nothing's down yet," Priya admitted.

When time ran out, every domino fell. Everyone at the table said cat. The judges guessed a swan, a fish, and a snake.
