# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Priya first pitched an owl, but Leo passed on the client's note that they were tired of owls and wanted something calm and wise, so the team settled on an elephant. Maya laid the start and kept the back, Leo took the head and trunk, and Tom worked the right side. Then the pieces snagged. Maya spotted an unfinished junction, Leo and Maya kept trading the head, and Tom laid the ear before admitting he had called it too soon. The real turning point came when Maya saw the back "climbs up to the wrong spot" and never reached the head. Leo connected the back to the start, Maya ran a straight line into the head, and Priya and Tom bridged the belly and the kink. Tom called "Looking good, team," and Maya kept working the head join. When the push came, 84 of 194 fell. Every builder said elephant, while the blind judges guessed rivers, roads and tree roots.
