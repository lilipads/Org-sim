# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

At a farm café, the team had a tight time limit to build a domino picture. Hana proposed a chicken, and Jack agreed, but Sofia raised the client's words, "no animals people eat," which ruled chicken out. Jack came round, and the team settled on a donkey, the animal the owner once kept. Omar wanted a cat, but Hana pointed out that the sketch was already done and the clock was ticking, so Omar joined the donkey plan.

Then the build hit trouble. Jack's back line dipped near the front, and Hana found a break that left everything past it unconnected. Hana joined the gap to the start while Sofia took the neck and head, since Omar had been heads-down on the back line. Omar found a back leg not standing and laid it again, then fixed a wobble in the belly. Sofia redid both ears, and at the end Hana nudged the chin into line. "Hands off everyone, let it fall!"

131 of 230 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a donkey, but the blind judges guessed a river, a winding road, or a coastline.
