# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

At a farm café table, four teammates raced to build a hen from dominoes. Hana started it: "Right, let's do a hen." Sofia flagged that the café is vegetarian. Hana kept the hen anyway.

The pieces came together slowly. Jack re-laid the right leg after its join showed too big a gap, and Sofia bridged the belly to the left leg. The back line was the hardest. Hana laid the top half from the tail while Omar laid the bottom half up from the leg, and they met in the middle.

Near the end, Jack spotted a dead end at the top right and offered a bridge. Hana stopped him: "Hands off the chain from here so nobody bumps it."

Omar wrote, "Here we go, hen, don't let us down!" The result: 150 of 166 fell. Each teammate named it a hen, but the blind judges guessed birds with long, thin beaks: songbirds, doves, and hummingbirds.
