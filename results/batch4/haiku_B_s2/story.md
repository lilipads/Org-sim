# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

At one table, four teammates tried to build a fish out of dominoes in the time allowed. Sofia sketched it with the nose on the left and the start at the nose. Hana had wanted the tail on the left, but she agreed when Jack backed Sofia's plan: "Nose on the left, start at the nose, fall runs right into the fin."

The first snag came at the nose, where Omar spotted that the bottom edge wasn't connected to the start and bridged it. Later he branched the eye off the top edge. Jack's fin kept curling past its tip, so he laid it again straight out to the point. Hana caught a sharp turn where the top edge met the fork, and Sofia eased it into a gentle curve.

The last gap was at the fork. Hana found that the top edge ended with nothing ahead of it, so Sofia closed that joint and Jack started the fin off her last domino. When Jack thought the fin was down, Hana saw it was still pale and closed the joint herself.

In the end, 40 of 63 dominoes fell. The team all called it a fish, but the blind judges saw a curved line, like a road or a wave.
