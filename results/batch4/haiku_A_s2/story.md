# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Priya began the sketch with a fish, saying, "Okay, let's do a fish!" The team agreed to start the run at the top of the tail, follow the back to the nose, and curve under the belly. Tom took the belly, Maya took the forked fin, and Leo fanned out the tail. Everyone needed the tail base, where the belly, the fork, and Priya's lower fin all had to meet.

That joint became the trouble spot. Tom's belly stopped short of it, and his retrace did not take. "Tom, stop retracing, you're chasing the same piece," Maya said, and she laid a bridge herself from the belly's end to her prongs. Leo's fan never joined the back, and his retrace placed nothing, so he stopped rather than risk bumping it.

Near the end, Tom spotted a domino knocked off the line at the prong end. Maya told him, "nudge it in now with a fix," and then the team kept their hands off the joint. When the start domino was pushed, 75 of 85 fell. Everyone said fish, but the blind judges saw a hook, a bird's neck, or a question mark.
