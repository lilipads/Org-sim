# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish against the clock. Priya sketched the plan: start at the tail tip and run both edges to the nose. Maya took the top edge, and Tom built the bottom one, bridging the gap at the nose tip.

The fins caused the most trouble. Leo and Priya both reached for the upper fin, and Priya wrote, "Leo, don't trace the upper fin." Leo apologized: "I should have checked the table before grabbing the fin." He re-laid it from the body to the tail, then spent the closing minutes bridging pale spots at the bend and tail tip while Priya stayed clear.

With time almost gone, Maya kept nudging the nose tip and asked Tom to keep his hands off it. Then came the final call: "We're out of time."

When the start domino fell, 93 of 112 went down. Everyone said fish. The blind judges guessed a bird, a boat, or a fish.
