# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Maya suggested a fish, and Priya sketched it on the pad. Leo laid the body lines, but the team soon hit trouble. Tom finished the long top line and found the start sitting at the body end, so the tail fell into it rather than out of it. He moved the start to the top of the tail and laid the line again. Priya spotted the belly running the wrong way, cleared it, and redrew it fresh on the pad. Maya found the tail bar stopping dead at the tip and bridged it, while Tom nudged the break there. With time almost gone, Leo nudged the nose and tail root joints and wrote, "That's all we can do, hands off everyone. Good fish, whatever falls." In the end, 70 of 145 fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the judges saw a river, a road, a swan.
