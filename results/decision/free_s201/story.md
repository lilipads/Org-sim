# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Priya started with a simple idea: a butterfly, since the client wanted something colourful that flies. The team agreed. Leo took the body, Priya the big upper wings, Maya a lower wing, and Tom the antennae.

Then the plan nearly stalled. Leo kept saying the body was going in, but the table showed only the upper wings. Priya spoke up with minutes left: "I'm not seeing the body down on the table yet." Leo apologised and tried to redraw the body from the bottom up. Maya spotted the real problem. The start domino falls upward, so a body running up into it could never be hit. She didn't wait. She hung her lower wing off the end of the upper wing, Priya did the same on the other side, and Leo scrapped his body. Tom branched the antennae off the wings, one per side.

Leo checked the chain, and everything connected to the start. When the last domino fell, all 139 went down. Everyone saw a butterfly, but the blind judges guessed everything from a bug to a bunny.
