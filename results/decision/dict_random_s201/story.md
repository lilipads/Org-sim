# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Priya called it early: a butterfly, for a client's new butterfly house. Leo took the body and its feelers, Maya laid the right wings, and Tom, who suggested mirror wings, took the left. Then the wings wouldn't catch. Priya kept finding gaps at the joint and bridging them, until Leo worked out the real problem: the wings left the body flat, and a falling domino couldn't turn that sharp a corner. "They need a little curved ramp out of the body," he said, and he built one on each side.

Late in the game, the team ran short of dominoes. Leo cleared stray loose pieces to free more for his bridges, and Tom lifted a jammed domino at the left ramp joint. Priya's early bridge sat too close to the body, so she asked Maya to lift it. With time nearly up, Tom stayed clear and cheered them on. "Hands off, everyone!"

Result: 97 of 250 fell. Everyone saw a butterfly, but the blind judges guessed plants and bare branches.
