# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At this table, Leo picked an elephant: "calm and wise, and the trunk makes the outline obvious." He sketched it and split the work. Maya took the back, head and outer trunk. Tom took the rump, legs and belly. Priya took the ear, but for a moment the inner trunk was hers too, until Leo took it himself.

The trouble was the joins. Tom asked to split the back so they could meet in the middle, but Leo refused: "Let's keep the back as one stroke." Gaps kept appearing at the top of the head, and Priya kept bridging them. Leo also cleared a stray run along the top right, because it "isn't in the plan."

With five minutes left, Maya offered a connect, but Leo ran it himself. Tom's legs and belly went dark first, and then the back followed. Only the inner ear stayed pale. Maya stopped her own connect so no one would bump at the head.

Everyone said elephant. The blind judges guessed a horse, a dog, a pony, or a rabbit. When the start was pushed, 200 of 236 fell.
