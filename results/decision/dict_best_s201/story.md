# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

## In short

It started with a client. Priya heard that the garden centre wanted something colourful that flies, so she suggested a butterfly, and Tom sketched a symmetrical body with wings on both sides. The jobs kept shifting, because Maya had already started the body. In the end Maya kept the body, Priya took the right wings, Leo the left, and Tom the feelers.

The first turning point came when Maya finished the body and saw that the right top wing wasn't up yet, so she laid it. Tom then found a gap where the top left wing stopped short of the body and closed it.

The last push was the right top wing. Leo took its bottom edge, Maya its top curve, and they met on the outer edge. Priya flagged a domino that looked too far, and Maya checked it. Tom noticed the loose ends, and Leo lifted a stray tail clear of the bridge. Then Tom called it: "the butterfly's symmetrical and ready to fly." Hands off.

When the start domino fell, 232 of 237 went down.
