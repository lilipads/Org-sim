## In short

It began with a disagreement. Leo caught that the client wanted a change from owls, so Priya dropped the idea. Maya heard that Biscuit, the shop cat, sleeps in that very window. Priya agreed: "Biscuit sleeping in that very window tips it for me."

Priya laid the curled tail, and Maya carried the right side down to meet it. When Maya asked, Tom took the flat base line. Leo noticed the head had no ear points and laid the ear strokes himself.

With minutes running out, Tom checked the joint where the right side met the base. A domino sat a touch off its path, so he nudged it into line. Leo confirmed, "From the start it all traces through." The team agreed: "Hands off from me until the push."

Priya said, "Go, Biscuit!" and 79 of 79 fell. The team all said cat, while blind judges saw boots, a bird's leg and a vase.
