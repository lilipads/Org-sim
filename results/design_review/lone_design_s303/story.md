## In short

Against the clock, Priya brought a request from the trail's client: a garden snail named Shelly, with a big spiral shell. Tom sketched it, and the team got to work.

The biggest turning point came in Maya's dry run. The shell spiral peeled off heading back toward the tail, so it never caught the fall. Tom redrew it to leave the body heading toward the head, and the neck eased up gradually. Leo's test then showed the feelers never caught, so Tom redrew them to start right at the top of the neck, going nearly straight up.

When the client asked for an eye dot, Maya pointed out that a separate dot "never falls," so the team skipped it. Tom's squint test said the figure "reads as a snail," "decent, not perfect." Everyone kept hands off the shell, and the team waited.

When the start domino was pushed, 110 of 110 fell. Everyone called it a snail, while the blind judges guessed snake, swan, and fishhook.
