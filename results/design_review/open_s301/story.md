## In short

Maya met the client and sketched a butterfly for a garden centre, with no birds allowed. Priya's plan was to start at the body and hang mirrored wing loops off it. Maya split the build among the four of them, and the clock started. When the build stalled, Priya took the body: "I'm grabbing the body now so we don't stall." Tom spotted the first gap: "the right antenna stops just short of the body." Maya closed it. Tom's inspection then flagged the pale joins, and Priya's dry run declared the wings falling. Her picture said otherwise, and she admitted, "I called it too early." Leo bridged the top left wing, but the dominoes ran out with the join still uncertain. Everyone kept their hands off. The push sent 164 of 250 dominoes falling. Each builder called it a butterfly, while the judges guessed scribbles, birds, and ribbons.
