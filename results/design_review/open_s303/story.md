# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

**The table**

## In short

Priya opened with a snail: a garden snail with a big spiral shell, a calm mascot the kids would love. Leo's advice shaped the plan: keep the spiral loose, leave the feelers at a shallow angle, and run a single line from the foot into the shell. Then the lines went down. Leo's dry run showed the foot breaking at a sharp bend near the head, so he redrew that end with a long, easy rise, and Tom's feelers hooked on from it. Priya had already laid past the old corner, so Leo built from where she ended. Maya carried the spiral down the left side. When Priya's line overlapped hers, Priya backed off. She then traced the inner coil from Maya's bottom sweep, as Leo had sketched it. Hands came off the table. When the push came, 167 of 167 fell. Every player called it a snail.
