# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

At the rowing club's table, the client's brief was clear: a bird from the lake, graceful, with no fish. Jack had met the client and mentioned the Silver Swan trophy, so the team settled on a swan. Hana sketched it, and the work was split up. Jack laid the belly, Sofia took the back and neck, and Omar watched the joins.

The first turning point came when Omar spotted a gap at the tail. Sofia checked and then corrected herself, admitting the tail join was fine. The real gap was higher up, where the neck stopped short of the curve and the head and beak were still missing.

Hana's squint check set the priority: "the head and beak are what make it a swan." Sofia carried the neck up over the head, and Jack ran the beak out to the right. When Jack inspected the finished run, it was good news: 103 of 104 dominoes fell, and the stray at the tail was harmless. "Hands off, everyone, and let's watch it fall!"
