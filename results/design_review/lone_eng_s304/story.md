# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

At a children's aquarium lobby, a team had twenty minutes to build a big, bold whale out of dominoes. Omar brought the client's brief, and Hana designed the whale and laid its back. Jack took the belly, while Sofia and Omar were both drawn to the flukes. Sofia bumped a domino near the tail, and the fall kept dying at the tail joint, where the turn was too sharp. Omar tried a curve, but Hana asked him to hold off while she inspected the run. She found the back cut off and joined it to the belly. Sofia carried the back toward the head, Jack brought the belly round to the nose, and Omar closed the arc. Everyone said "whale," but the head stayed open, and the blind judges guessed a curved line, a loop, a road, or a speech bubble. Final result: 94 of 221 fell.
