# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Omar asked for a whale: big, bold, and readable across a children's aquarium lobby. Sofia sketched one, and Jack warned that its little spout would never fall, so she slanted it into a low branch. Jack then found the tail lobes left the crossing too steeply and laid a short stem with two lobes forking off it. Hana's dry run showed the top and belly lines stopping short, so she extended both to meet at the crossing. Sofia's squint check read the spout as a fish, so she lifted it and let the fluke carry the whale. Hana's inspection of the real table found the tail sound, and Omar carried the upper lobe up to match the lower one. With hands off and popcorn out, the team let the push go. "Go little whale!" Sofia called. All 117 dominoes fell, and all three blind judges guessed a fish.
