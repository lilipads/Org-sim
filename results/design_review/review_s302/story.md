## In short

A swan for a rowing club by a lake: that was the brief. Jack met the client, who wanted a graceful bird with a long, elegant neck, and Hana drafted it in side view. Jack's review asked for a slimmer, taller S-curve, and Hana took every note. When the client loved the swan but found the neck thick and stubby, Hana redrew the neck and took it on herself.

Sofia laid the back line while Omar built the front line from the start. The turning point came when the team learned that nothing would fall without the start domino at the tail. Hana reminded Omar, "start domino first please," and he put it in. Sofia and Jack then hunted for breaks near the tail, nudging dominoes back into line.

Time ran short. Jack asked for "tiny fixes only," and Omar kept laying the front line. When the clock ran out, 9 of 111 dominoes fell. Everyone still called it a swan.
