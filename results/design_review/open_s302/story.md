## In short

Jack came back from meeting the client with a brief: a graceful bird for a rowing club wall by a lake, with the Silver Swan as their proudest trophy. "So I say swan!" The team agreed, and Hana asked for one smooth S-curve neck.

The first sketch became the turning point. Hana's squint check said it "reads as a shrimp, not a swan," so she took the pad and redrew only the neck as a taller S with a clear head and beak. Omar traced the rear neck, Sofia carried the front neck up to the head, and Hana took the lower half of the rear neck when the clock got tight.

The wing kept breaking where it met the back line, so Sofia re-laid that joint with a gentler branch. Omar bridged the gap at the head and laid one curve into the beak. In the final moments, he wrote, "Hands off, everyone. Fingers crossed, let the swan swim!"

Result: 150 of 180 fell. The team all called it a swan, but the blind judges guessed a banana, a scythe, a bean pod, or a hook.
