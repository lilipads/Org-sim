# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Maya's client wanted a symmetrical butterfly for a garden centre entrance, with no birds. Leo drew one with wings and feelers on top. Then Tom ran the fall and found a problem: the top wings left the body nearly sideways, so the run could never turn that sharp. Leo redrew them to sweep up and outward, and the team locked it in.

With the clock running, Priya laid the body and start while Tom and Maya worked the wings. Priya asked everyone to keep clear of the middle line, warning that re-tracing would lift what she had laid. Her dry runs kept stopping at the same place: only the left feeler would fall. Maya offered to carry the body down from where it ended, and Priya said yes, "only extend, no re-trace." A gap below the start line stalled the fall, and Priya and Maya worked to close it.

When time ran out, every player still called it a butterfly. The blind judges guessed a chain, a bent cane, or a fishing hook. Final result: 20 of 124 fell.
