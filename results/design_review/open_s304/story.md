# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Omar pitched a whale for a kids' aquarium entrance: big, bold, and no sharks. Sofia sketched it nose-left, but Jack's dry run caught sharp angles and a thin tail. Sofia redrew it his way, and the team split up: Jack the back, Hana the belly, Omar and Sofia the flukes.

The flukes failed first. Hana spotted that they left the tail too steeply and drew gentler replacements, which Omar and Sofia built.

The belly was the real trouble. It looked pale at the nose, so Hana re-laid it from the start while Jack carried it from the tail. Sofia's stray line drifted off, and Hana pulled it down. Omar bridged the belly near the middle.

Hana's final dry run showed the belly, back, stalk and both flukes falling from the start. She cleared the stray run below the middle, and everyone kept their hands off. "Let it fall!"

Result: 142 of 159 fell. Everyone called it a whale, but the blind judges mostly saw a fish or a leaf.
