## In short

Omar opened with a whale: "big, friendly, and it breathes air, so no sharks." He handed out the strokes, and Hana ran the dry runs. Her first check found the spout peeling off the first line at too steep an angle, so Omar redrew it to leave earlier and curl up. The next check caught the upper tail fluke doing the same. Omar redrew it as a longer, straighter run that curls only at the tip, and it finally fell. Jack carried the belly up to the tail, where a small kink sat at the corner. Hana fixed it while everyone kept their hands off. Sofia warned that the tail and spout could read as a pumpkin, and asked for the flukes to be spread wide and clear. In the end, 101 of 110 fell. The four players all said whale, but the blind judges guessed fish, swan, bird, and seahorse.
