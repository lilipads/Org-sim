# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya sketched a snail for the pond trail: a long body line, a spiral shell, and a head with feelers. Leo's dry runs showed the body and shell falling, but the feelers kept missing because the neck left the head too sharply. Maya redrew the neck to carry straight up, with longer feelers, and the client confirmed it was Shelly. With the clock running down, Maya locked the drawing and split the work: Leo took the body, Tom the shell, and Priya the right feeler.

The body stopped short of the feelers, so Priya bridged up to the right one. Tom finished the shell's inner curl. Maya fixed a jammed spot at the base of the right feeler and a kink near its top, while Leo bridged the neck into the feeler's base. Then Maya called, "everyone hands off," and Tom said, "Let it fall!"

Result: 109 of 110 fell. Everyone on the team had called it a snail, and the blind judges mostly guessed snails or curled shells too.
