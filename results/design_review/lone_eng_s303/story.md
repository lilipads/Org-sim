## In short

On a pond trail, a team set out to build a domino picture of Shelly, the client's garden snail. Priya brought the brief: slow, calm, and a big spiral. Maya sketched a long body, a spiral shell, and a head with feelers.

Leo's dry run was the warning sign. The feelers never caught. Maya redrew the neck and set each feeler right on the neck line, but the run kept missing them.

With time running out, Priya urged the team on: "Lock it when you're ready, even if the feelers are imperfect." Maya locked the sketch and planned to extend the feelers by hand. Leo laid the body, Tom built the shell, and Priya queued both feelers while Maya stood by.

Then the run carried all the way through. Priya nudged a loose domino back into line, and everyone kept their hands off. "Hands off, and let Shelly go!" Priya said. The whole snail fell: 107 of 107 dominoes.
