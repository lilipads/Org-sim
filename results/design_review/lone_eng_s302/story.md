## In short

**The Swan That Nearly Wasn't**

Sofia sketched the swan as lines from the tail: one over the back, one round the belly. Omar took the back, neck and beak, and Hana took the belly. Jack returned from the client with news that they loved the swan but worried the body looked lopsided. Sofia kept the shape, since both strokes had dry-run clean. Jack carried the back to the bend, Sofia raised the neck, and Omar took the head. Then Hana's squint became the turning point. The shape still read as a banana, she said, and she asked for a bigger beak. Omar stretched it into a long pointed wedge, and Sofia closed a small gap at the head. Hana reported the beak wasn't catching yet, but Omar's dry run said it fell clean, so the team agreed to leave it alone. When the push came, all 107 fell. Everyone said swan. Blind judges guessed swan or goose, shrimp or prawn, boot or slipper.
