- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Jack sketched a swan for the Silver Swan trophy and split the strokes: Sofia on the belly, Omar on the back, Hana on the neck and head, and Jack on the wing. Early on, Sofia admitted her dry run had not actually finished, and Jack confessed he had never pressed lock. Those corrections kept the team honest.

The turning point came at the back. Omar, judging by eye, said the neck looked too steep, and Sofia asked Hana to hold off on the neck join until she had checked it. The back became the bottleneck, so Jack helped carry it across. Sofia fell behind on the belly, then found it doubled in the middle and left it alone rather than risk breaking the run.

Omar carried the neck up to the head, and Hana laid the beak last, the only piece still missing. Sofia's final dry run showed everything falling from the start, and the team agreed: "Hands off, everyone."

In the end, 123 of 123 fell. The team saw a swan, while blind judges guessed a goose, a leaf, and a boat.
