# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Maya met the client, a garden centre launching a butterfly house, and came back with a clear brief: a symmetrical butterfly, and no birds. Tom sketched two mirrored wings from one centre point and locked the design. Priya's first dry run showed the antennae wouldn't catch, so Tom redrew them. Leo laid the left antenna, Maya the right, and the centre finally caught. The second dry run ran clean. Tom took the lower curl on the right, but Priya spotted a loose join where the curl met the wing, and Tom bridged it. Maya then spotted a spur running past the turn, and Tom pulled it back. Leo carried the left curl, then the right, up to the tip. "Let it stand," the team agreed, and the blind judges saw a butterfly too. Hands off, they let it fall: 170 of 170 dominoes.
