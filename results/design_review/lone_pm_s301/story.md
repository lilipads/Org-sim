## In short

A garden centre wanted a butterfly for its entrance, with no birds after pigeon trouble. Tom took the body, and Priya and Leo took the wings. Tom asked Maya to hold off locking, because the wings came off "almost flat sideways" and wouldn't catch. Maya redrew them to rise steeply before curling out.

Priya then found that the lower wings left the body "too flat down by the bottom." Maya redrew those too, and soon Priya reported, "Both new lower wings catch off the body." With time short, Maya traced the antennae as drawn, since "even a partial fall helps it read as a butterfly."

The last snag was at the head join, where Tom found one domino that "sits off its path" and cut off the right wing. Priya bridged that join while the others kept their hands off the middle. Maya called, "Hands off, let it run." In the end, 82 of 146 dominoes fell. The team saw a butterfly, but the blind judges guessed a bare tree.
