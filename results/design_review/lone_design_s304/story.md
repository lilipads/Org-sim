# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team set out to build a whale for a children's aquarium lobby. Sofia drew the design, but Hana spotted the first big problem: the start domino sat back by the old stem, so the fall could not reach the whole shape. Sofia moved the start to the nose and blunted it, as Hana suggested.

Jack laid the belly and kept testing the real table. His sketch run came back clean, but he admitted, "my earlier clean result was on the sketch, not the real dominoes." The belly stayed pale at the nose until he found the join leaving too steeply and bridged it with a gentle connector.

Hana carried the top line to the tail. Omar took the spout and a fluke, and Sofia took the lower fluke. Near the end, Hana closed the nose gap while Sofia told the others, "Hands off from me, you two are on it. Let it fly!"

The result: 85 of 188 fell. Every teammate called it a whale, but the blind judges guessed a fish, a snake, or a bird.
