# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The client wanted something colourful that flies and visits flowers, with no birds. Maya chose a butterfly, Leo drafted it, and Tom and Priya tightened the wings so every piece would join the outline.

Maya laid the body and antennae, and Leo took the lower wings. Then the upper wings became a tangle. Several people started the same ones. "If we both do it we'll lift each other's dominoes," Priya warned. Her dry run showed the upper wings never caught, because they left the body too sideways.

Tom sketched steeper replacements that climbed the body before bowing out. Maya traced the right wing, and Leo the left. Leo's inner end sat on the wrong line, but Tom reported it still caught through the lower line. Maya laid the last piece as the clock ran down.

Result: 144 of 155 fell. Everyone at the table said butterfly, and every blind judge saw one too, with a few extra guesses like flowers and a bow tie.
