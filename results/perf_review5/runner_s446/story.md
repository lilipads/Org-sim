## In short

Jack proposed a fish, big and simple, with two lines leaving the nose and pinching at the tail. He sketched it on the pad while Hana kept the bin stocked and reminded the team that the bottom line would only start once the top reached the nose. Omar offered to take the bottom line, but Sofia was already queued for it. "Stay on patching the top," she told him, and he did. Once the top reached the nose, Sofia rolled the bottom line down the body. It stopped short of the tail, so Omar carried it to the tip. Sofia spotted pale strays at the nose that weren't connected and cleared them away. Then everyone kept their hands off. When the push came, "Go, fish, go!" and the whole fish fell: 101 of 101 dominoes. Blind judges, seeing only the result, all guessed fish.
