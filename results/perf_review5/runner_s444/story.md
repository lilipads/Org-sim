*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Jack sketched a fish on the pad and handed out the strokes. Sofia kept the bin full, Hana took the top line, and Omar took the bottom. Jack started the top line himself while Hana was out at the boxes.

The trouble came at the tail. Hana spotted the loose bit at the bend: "that turn is pretty sharp, so the fall won't carry round it." Jack lifted the pale tip and re-laid it as a smooth curve, rounding it off step by step. Then he found it sat a touch off line and nudged it in.

Hana checked the whole run from the start: "the whole run carries through, nose to both tail points. Looks like a fish to me." Jack flicked the tail out into a fork, and the team agreed: hands off.

When the push came, 113 of 113 dominoes fell. Blind judges guessed a fish.
