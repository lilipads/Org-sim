## In short

Priya sketched a fish facing left and split the work. Tom and Maya fetched dominoes for the bin, Leo took a tray for himself, and each person claimed a part: the top line, the bottom line, or the tail. Priya laid the top line and the start domino, but it wouldn't catch. "Top line's laid but it isn't hooked to the start yet," she noted. She pulled the first top domino at the nose and hooked the line back in.

The turning point came when Tom bridged the gap with a gentle curve. "Good news: the run's catching now," Priya wrote, and nearly all of it fell from the start. Leo finished the lower tail, Maya completed the bottom line, and the team agreed to keep their hands off the nose. When the facilitator's push came, 88 of 93 dominoes fell. Everyone had called it a fish, and the blind judges agreed, though some saw a bird.
