# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish in twenty minutes. Maya proposed it and took the top line, while Priya sketched the pad and took the bottom. Tom and Leo kept the bin stocked. The bottom line nearly stalled when Priya's stroke "didn't land on the pad," so she redrew it, and Tom and Priya agreed to split the belly and back half at the deepest point. Tom then fixed the join at the start so the bottom line would fall too. Near the end, Maya found the sharp curl at the upper lobe and nudged it, while Leo closed a gap just before the lower fork's tip. Priya looked the whole run over: "the whole run falls from the start." Everyone kept hands off. At the push, all 143 dominoes fell.
