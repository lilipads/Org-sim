## In short

Four teammates set out to draw a fish with dominoes. Hana suggested the idea, and the jobs were split: Jack on the top line, Sofia on the bottom, Omar on the tail, and Hana on the fins and eye. Then the lines began to collide. Hana and Sofia were both tracing the bottom, and Sofia stopped hers: "Good catch, Hana."

Trouble gathered at the nose. Sofia's small bridges kept failing, so she re-laid the top line from the start. Hana caught a bigger snag: "the table says there's no start domino right now." Omar's tail kept stopping at the sharp turn, so he asked for the pad. Jack switched to laying the top line backwards from the tail joint while Sofia built forward from the nose.

With the clock nearly gone, Hana and Omar kept nudging the same joints, and Sofia asked everyone to keep clear: "hands off the nose and the middle now." When the push came, 54 of 85 dominoes fell. Everyone at the table said it was a fish. The blind judges saw a curve, a swoosh, and a rope.
