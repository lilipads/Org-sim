# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team chose a fish. Maya sketched it and started the top line, Tom took the bottom, and Priya claimed the tail while Leo watched the joins. When Maya noticed the tail looked pale, Priya found the cause: the start sat at the body, so the tail ran the wrong way. She began re-laying it from the top tip while the others waited at the junction.

Time ran short. Leo and Maya both flagged weak spots on the lower fin and the bottom corner, and Priya kept those fixes in her own batch. Tom stepped in to lay the tail himself, but Priya stopped him: "Tom, hold on, don't lay it!" Her batch then failed to land, so she started again and handed Maya the lower fin.

Near the end, Tom laid the tail from the pad, and Priya answered, "Tom, yes, go!" 70 of 74 dominoes fell. Blind judges guessed almonds, leaves and lemons, not a fish.
