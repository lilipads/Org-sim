# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The table set out to draw a fish, and everyone agreed on it. Maya took the pad first, laying the body from the tail tip while Leo traced the belly along her top edge. Tom waited to add his dorsal fin, and Priya planned a V-notch tail. Then the plan stalled. Maya kept redrawing, and the pad kept changing hands. "The redraw's eating time," Priya warned. Late on, the belly still hadn't joined the start. Maya took the tail joint herself, Leo stayed clear, and Tom patched his loose fin. "We're nearly out of time," Maya wrote. When the facilitator pushed the start domino, only the connected pieces fell. The blind judges guessed ridges and zigzags, not fish. In the end, 34 of 75 fell.
