# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Jack proposed a fish, and the team agreed: a big tail and an eye. Hana set the plan. The top line would start at the nose, the belly would meet it at the tail, and Omar would take the fin. Sofia built the belly.

Trouble came at the nose. The belly looked pale, and Jack warned, "The belly line Sofia built isn't hooked to the start yet." His lead-in kept going down only halfway. Meanwhile, Omar's fin kept dropping just the joint domino, so he split it into upper and lower strokes.

Hana found that the fin's lines met, but nothing stood past the joint. Jack moved the start to its far left end and rebuilt the nose. Hana spotted a domino off to the side, and Jack cleared the nose. When the belly still refused to catch, Sofia re-laid it to the tail.

Everyone said fish, but the blind judges saw a comet, a tadpole, or a swoosh. Final result: 105 of 108 fell.
