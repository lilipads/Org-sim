# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Four teammates had twenty minutes to build a domino fish. Sofia sketched the body, Jack claimed the fiddly forked tail, and Hana offered to trace the belly from the tail end. Omar, who had sketched a fish of his own, passed over the pad and waited.

The first turning point came when Sofia admitted she had said the fork was on the pad when it wasn't: "Sorry, Jack, I said the fork was on the pad when it wasn't." Jack and Hana kept waiting, and the belly never appeared. The pad kept changing hands as each person offered it to someone else.

Near the end, Jack took the pad himself, saying it "saves passing it about." Hana set the pad down and kept her hands off the table. Omar bridged the dorsal fin's base onto the top line, and Sofia closed the nose gap by hand.

When time ran out, 40 of 49 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed a hook, a tadpole, or a teardrop.
