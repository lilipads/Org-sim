# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

At a table, a team set out to build a domino picture of an animal. Sofia grabbed the pad and proposed a fish: "Simple side view, the outline does the work." The turning point came when Hana noticed the tail was just a point. "The tail fork is what will say fish," she said, and Sofia redrew it with a notch. Hana traced the top line from the tail tip to the nose while Omar took the bottom edge. Jack kept his hands off until the go. Late on, Omar spotted that the bottom edge wasn't linked to the start. "The fork has to meet it right at the tail," he said, and he closed that join himself. When time was up, the start domino at the top tail tip was pushed. Forty-two of 91 fell. Everyone said fish, but the blind judges guessed a wavy line, a winding river, or a curved row of fallen dominoes.
