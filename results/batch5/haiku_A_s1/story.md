# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Maya sketched a fish on the pad, with a forked tail and a notch where the tail splits. "The forked tail is what makes it read as a fish," she said. Tom laid the first stroke from the tail tip to the notch and along the belly. Maya took the back, and Leo worked the lower tail arm.

The notch became the trouble spot. Tom and Maya both flagged it as the weak joint, so Priya stepped in to steady it. Inspection after inspection showed loose dominoes right where Leo's arm met the notch. "Still closing those at the notch join," Priya kept calling, and everyone else kept their hands off.

As time ran short, Maya made the call: "I'm dropping the back." Tom set the eye just behind the nose, and Leo moved his arm toward the tail tip. Priya worked the join until the end.

When the start domino was pushed, 29 of 67 fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges saw a chevron, an arrow, and a bent road.
