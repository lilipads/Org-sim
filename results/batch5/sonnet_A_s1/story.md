# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

With time running down, the team's fish took shape. Priya had promised a sketch, but the pad was blank, so Maya drew the fish herself and split the work. Priya took the top body, Tom the bottom body and upper fin, and Maya the lower fin. Leo stepped aside, and Priya took the back edge.

The first turning point came when Tom and Maya spotted pale strays curling past the fin tip. Priya asked Maya to clear them, but Tom noticed they were already gone: "the strays past the fin tip are already gone."

The second came when the back edge would not hook in at the top. Leo had said, "if it won't connect, it's fine to leave it out." Priya did exactly that, insisting, "The fish is connected from the start through the body, tail and both fins."

When the start domino fell, 102 of 120 dominoes went with it, and every teammate had seen a fish.
