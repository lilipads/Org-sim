## In short

Chloe opened the table with a simple plan: a fish swimming right, with the chain starting at the tail. Ava took the top line, Ben the belly and head, and Dev the tail and fin. The first trouble came when Dev found the start sitting well away from where Chloe had placed it, with a stray run of dominoes across the tail's path. Ava confirmed the run wasn't hers, and Dev cleared it. Later, Ava's top line turned out to run away from the tail, so Chloe kept it as "a separate stroke for the picture." Ben's arc was stalled by a first domino that faced the wrong way, so he lifted it and re-placed it. Ava laid the bridge that joined the arc to Chloe's fin. Dev's final inspection showed the chain carrying through only a short stretch, breaking at a sharp turn, and no one trusted a fix in time. At the push, 12 of 148 fell. The blind judges guessed a squiggle or a row of fallen dominoes, not a fish.
