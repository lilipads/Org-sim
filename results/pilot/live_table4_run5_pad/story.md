# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Ava**: You're a hands-on engineer who would rather show than tell.
- **Ben**: You're a product designer with strong taste.
- **Chloe**: You're calm and naturally see the big picture.
- **Dev**: You're careful and detail-oriented, and you'd rather check twice than redo something.

## In short

The team of four set out to draw a fish in a timed round. Ben sketched the outline and laid the bottom edge, Chloe took the top, Ava built the tail, and Dev offered to help on the back. Early on, Dev's bump knocked a domino down, and Chloe had to stand it back up. Then the top edge stalled behind one domino. Chloe kept saying it was "nearly up" until Ben asked, "Is it actually standing?" Chloe admitted her hands had been idle. The team agreed to route around it. Dev took the top edge, and Ava joined her tail onto Chloe's start instead. Late in the round, Ava's join to the bottom edge kept failing. Ben's inspection found the chain dying at a sharp turn, cutting the bottom edge off from the start. As time ran out, everyone kept their hands still. Ten of 247 dominoes fell. The team all saw a fish, but the blind judges guessed a letter y, a twig, or a slingshot.
