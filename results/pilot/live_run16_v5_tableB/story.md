## In short

Hana opened the table with a fish: an oval body, a triangle tail, and a dot for the eye. Sofia took the top, Omar the belly, and Jack the tail and eye. Soon the pieces were tangled. Omar's bottom line faced the wrong way, so he pulled it up and rebuilt it. Jack's tail stayed pale, and he found that "the turn into the tail is too sharp." Late in the round, Sofia discovered the real problem: the run from the start stopped short at the nose, and the belly "starts behind the start domino, so nothing ever pushes it." With the team out of dominoes, Hana freed spares from the left side and stopped her own bridge so Sofia could finish. At the last minute Sofia called, "Go now!" Hana linked the start into the belly. When the start was pushed, only five of 128 fell. Everyone still saw a fish, but the blind judges saw rings, ovals, and smiles.
