# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You work in accounts payable and joined the company about a year ago.
- **Tom**: You've been in customer support for ten years and know everyone.
- **Priya**: You lead a small sales team.
- **Leo**: You're a data analyst.

## In short

The table agreed on a fish. Tom proposed it, Priya sketched the outline, and Tom ran the top edge from the nose while Maya took the bottom edge. Leo checked the tail crossing before anyone placed a domino: "Crossing's clean."

Trouble gathered at the nose. Priya spotted loose dominoes near the start, and Maya closed them, only to find the join still open. Tom gave Leo the go-ahead for the upper fin once he was past the middle. A stray domino on the top edge sat off to one side. Leo nudged it without checking, and Priya's continuity check confirmed the break. With spares running low, Priya took the fork nudge herself so Leo could keep his hands off. Maya was still tying in the nose when the log ends.

Everyone still said fish, but the blind judges guessed a chain bending at a corner. Result: 36 of 130 fell.
