# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

## In short

Omar opened with a bold call: a fish, with a big body, a triangle tail, and one googly eye. Hana backed it, and Sofia split the work. Jack ran the belly curve from the nose, Sofia took the top curve, and Hana built the tail. Omar added the eye on a gentle spur and bridged it into Sofia's top line, because, as he put it, "Fish needs to see where it's going."

The turning point came at the tail. Omar's detective report found breaks there, and Sofia fixed the joint until the tail looked dark. Hana then carried the bottom lip across to Jack's belly. Omar ran a full check from the nose while everyone kept their hands off the table.

When the push came, the chain dropped 112 of 115 dominoes. Only the pale leftovers by the brow stayed standing, so the fish had no unibrow. The team called it a fish. The blind judges saw a fish, a bird, a bag, and more.
