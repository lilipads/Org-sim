## In short

Ben opened with a plan for a fish: one continuous outline, from the tail tip along the top to the nose and back along the belly. Chloe agreed and split the work. Ben moved the start to the tail tip, facing right. Chloe built the top arc, Ava laid the belly facing left, and Dev caught the wrong-facing pieces and rounded the nose. The pieces wouldn't join. Ava's belly ran short of the tail, so Ben's tail had nothing to connect from until Ava carried it on. Then Ben found the tail broken, and with the clock running he stopped the repair: "the push should still carry the outline." Chloe and Ava spent the last minutes closing gaps in the arc and the kink. When the start was pushed, only 15 of 186 fell. Blind judges guessed a hook, an L, or a bent stroke, not a fish.
