# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You work in accounts payable and joined the company about a year ago.
- **Grace**: You run facilities for the office.
- **Sam**: You're a summer intern, a few weeks in.
- **Leo**: You're a data analyst.

## In short

Four teammates set out to build a fish from dominoes in twenty minutes. Grace started at the tail and laid the body running left, then split it so Maya could take the top, Sam the bottom, and Leo the tail and eye.

The split kept breaking. Grace and Leo crossed wires more than once, and at 4:45 Grace handed Leo the split joint with "fine, you take the split joint for Sam's line." Sam's bottom line stayed pale, and he cleared a jammed cluster in the middle, which Grace said pulled four of her dominoes out of the bridge.

The turning point came at the nose. At 12:00 Sam spotted the gap: "The bottom line's end points up-left, and your line ends pointing down just above it." Leo laid one curved join across it. Grace's last bridge went in at 18:45, and Maya's look over the table showed the top, body and tail falling, though the bottom line was still pale.

When time ran out, 122 of 180 dominoes fell. Every player called it a fish, but the blind judges saw a closed loop, a river map, or an island outline.
