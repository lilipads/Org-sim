# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You work in accounts payable and joined the company about a year ago.

- **Priya**: You lead a small sales team.
- **Leo**: You're a data analyst.

## In short

**A Fish, Built Domino by Domino**

The team agreed on a fish fast: a big body, a forked tail, and a single eye, all drawn as one closed outline. Maya laid the top curve from the nose, Priya the bottom, Tom the forked tail, and Leo the eye. When the scratch pad was taken away, they worked from memory, calling out "Shout when they're close" as the lines met.

The eye ring stayed stubbornly pale. Maya cleared the bunched dominoes on its right side, and Priya fixed the upper tail lobe that wouldn't go dark. Then Leo bumped the middle of the ring, and the table ran short of spares. Tom's inspection showed the body and tail solid.

At the push, the fish fell: 133 of 178 dominoes. Every builder said "fish." The blind judges guessed a vine, a rope, and a scribble.
