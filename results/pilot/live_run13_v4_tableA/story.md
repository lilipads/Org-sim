# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You work in IT security.
- **Tom**: You're an accountant.
- **Priya**: You're a civil engineer who moved into an operations role.
- **Leo**: You work in sales.

## In short

Four people set out to build a fish from dominoes in twenty minutes. Priya laid the lead-in line, Maya ran the top curve, Tom carried the bottom curve round the belly, and Leo built the tail and the eye. The plan was clear, but the run kept breaking. Priya bumped the table near the split early on, and Tom knocked the tail end later.

The nose became the biggest problem. Priya warned that "the whole top hangs off it," and Maya spent much of the game on that gap, asking the others to stay off it. Soon the team ran out of dominoes, so Priya and Tom pulled up strays that couldn't connect and reused them. Leo pulled one of Maya's by mistake, then admitted, "that was my mistake."

As time ran out, Leo was still closing the arm joint, and Tom waited to hook in the down line. "Hands off, fingers crossed!" The push came, and 75 of 180 fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the judges saw a river network, a bird, and a checkmark.
