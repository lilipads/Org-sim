# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 20 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're a software engineer.
- **Omar**: You work in HR.
- **Hana**: You're in marketing.
- **Jack**: You're a lawyer.

## In short

Sofia suggested a fish: "Big round body, triangle tail, super recognizable." Hana took the bottom curve, Omar the top arc, and Jack the tail, while Sofia started the nose and body. Hana wanted everyone to "aim at the same shape," and for a while the lines grew steadily along the body.

Then the joins started to matter. Hana's bottom wasn't connected to the nose, and the top looked pale where Sofia's line met Omar's start. Omar found the first top domino sat at too sharp an angle and bridged the neck. Sofia flagged a long line running up-left, but Hana asked to hold off clearing it until Omar's arc was connected. Bridges kept going the wrong way and had to be pulled back. Near the end, Jack's tail ran off course, and the middle line stopped just short of it.

Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges saw a plant. Only 39 of 180 fell.
