## In short

Maya sketched a fish on the pad: a big simple body, nose on the left, tail fan on the right. The team shared out the strokes. Priya took the top line, Tom the bottom line, Leo the lower tail lobe, and Maya the upper one.

Trouble started at the nose. Tom's bottom line would not catch, and Priya patched it. Then Tom's bridge overshot, so he took the stray piece down and redid the join. Priya spotted the real break, a wide gap between the start domino and the top line, and closed it. Maya offered to lay the far half of the belly, and Leo took the middle.

Near the crossing, Priya warned that the last stretch had to come in gently from the lower left. Tom nudged the final gap shut. Leo checked the fall, and the whole chain lit up from the start. "Hands off, everyone," Priya said. "Fish, swim!"

153 of 153 fell.
