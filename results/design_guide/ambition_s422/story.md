# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya proposed a swan, with a long curvy neck and a round body, because "nobody else will do it." Priya took the pad, sketched it, and laid the belly and start. Tom carried the back of the neck up over the head toward the beak. Maya built a wing that "hooks off the belly and the whole run still falls."

The turning point came when Leo spotted a risk: "the head line up top runs to the right, so curve the front neck gently toward the beak as it climbs." Maya curved the front of the neck in, finished it, and checked the beak joint. Then the team agreed: "Hands off," and everyone kept clear of the head and neck.

At the push, the swan went down. Blind judges guessed a swan or duck in profile, and every domino fell: 112 of 112.
