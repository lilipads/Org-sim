# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.

## In short

Maya suggested a fish because "it's an easy outline to read." Priya sketched it on the pad, and the team split the work: Maya took the top of the body, Tom the bottom, and Leo the tail. Priya watched the joints, and when she started the lower fin, she backed off after Leo said he already had it queued.

The turning point came at the back. Maya's top line was still short of the tail, and her link into the upper tail didn't take. She moved to redo it, but Leo called out, "hang on before you redo that link." Priya traced the tail and found it ran all the way from the start. Maya agreed the whole tail would fall and left her redo alone.

As time ran low, everyone kept their hands off. "Hands off, let it fall!" Maya wrote. The push came, and the fish fell: 106 of 106.
