# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team chose a fish at the start. Priya sketched the outline on the pad and split the work: Maya took the top of the body, Tom the bottom, and Leo the tail. Maya suggested starting the line at the nose so everything stayed connected, and the group agreed.

Trouble came with the lower fin. Priya found it "facing the wrong way" and laid it again, while Tom noticed jammed dominoes and a gap in the middle. Leo offered to re-lay the fin from the bottom corner "all at once," and Priya stepped back to let him.

With time running out, the belly stayed pale. Maya bridged the nose, and Tom announced he was bridging it too, so they ended up on the same spot. Priya asked Tom to step back. "Hands off everyone, let the nose and fin settle," she said. In the end, 89 of 177 fell. "Fingers crossed, it's a fish either way."
