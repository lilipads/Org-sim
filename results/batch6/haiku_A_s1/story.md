# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.

- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya sketched a fish and set the start at the nose, so both body lines would run the same way. Tom laid the top edge, Leo carried it on toward the tail, and Priya ran the bottom edge while Tom took the eye. Leo built the bottom fork and thought it was joined at the junction.

Then Maya looked closely and found the real trouble: the lower fork was pale, and the bottom body stopped just short of its base. Priya stretched the join across. Maya took the off-side domino at the fork base, while Leo traced the break to the top arm's lowest domino at the hub.

Late on, the inspection still showed both forks pale, so only the body was connected. Priya was fixing the lower base, Maya was on the top arm, and Leo was watching. "Shout if it still looks pale when the push comes," Priya said.

Final result: 95 of 123 fell. The team all said fish. The blind judges guessed fish, leaf, and eye.
