**The table**

## In short

Sofia pitched a fish with a big triangle tail, "easy to recognise." Jack sketched it, Sofia took the bottom half, and Omar took the tail.

The nose proved hardest. The bottom arc wouldn't hook to the start, and Hana fixed the joint twice before admitting, "it's not fixed." Sofia suggested the arc left the start too steeply, but Hana found a domino jammed too close past the start, pulled it, and bridged into the arc. Jack announced he was bridging from the nose too, until Hana asked him to hold off.

At the tail, Sofia fixed a jammed domino where the top arc ended, and Jack bridged a gap at the back edge himself. Near the end, Hana re-traced the bottom arc while everyone else stayed clear.

Time ran out. The team said it was a fish. 63 of 220 dominoes fell, and the blind judges guessed a winding river or road.
