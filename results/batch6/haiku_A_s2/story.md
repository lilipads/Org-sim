# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At this table, the team chose a fish. Maya sketched the body first, starting at the tail pinch and laying the top edge with its dorsal fin. Tom took the bottom edge, Priya took the tail, and Leo would add the pectoral fin.

Near the middle, Priya spotted a problem: "I can't see a stroke 3 on the pad, only the body." Leo agreed there was "no tail shape yet," then sketched the forked tail. Maya carried the top edge on to the nose tip so Tom's bottom edge would have something to meet.

Late in the round, the nose join drew the most attention. Maya, Tom, and Priya each checked it, and Tom traced the eye last. Then everyone kept their hands off.

When the push came, all 95 of 95 dominoes fell. Every player saw a fish, while the blind judges guessed a bird, a boat, and a hat.
