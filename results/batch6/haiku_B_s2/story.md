# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish. Sofia started the run at the top tip of the tail and carried the back toward the nose, while Jack traced the belly to meet it. Omar took the fin and the eye, and Hana worked the lower tail corner. "Honest call: the lower fin corner still isn't setting," she wrote, so she retraced the fin from the pad instead.

The turning point came when Omar's eye join turned too sharply at the top edge. He patched it while Sofia walked the chain from tail to nose, nudging pieces into place. Hana admitted the fin would not be finished in time: "the fin and the eye will have to carry the read."

When the push came, 100 of 141 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed a fish with a forked tail, though some saw a canoe or a leaf.
