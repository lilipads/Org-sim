## In short

Jack opened with a plan: a fish with a big body and a clear tail fin. He sketched it on the pad and split the strokes. Sofia took the top of the body, Hana the back of the tail, and Omar the eye, while Jack started the first domino by the tail and rolled the belly toward the nose.

The first turn came when Omar's eye line ran past the nose toward the edge. Sofia spotted it and asked him to stop there. Omar replied, "You're right, Sofia," and pulled the stretch back so the nose stayed clean.

Later the belly stalled short of the nose. Hana offered to take the last stretch, and Jack asked her to work from the nose end back toward him. Once the belly was in, Jack checked the nose join, left the crowded dominoes alone, and everyone kept their hands off. The push sent 144 of 153 falling, and all three blind judges guessed fish.
