# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Sofia opened with a plan: a fish, built from the nose back along the body. Hana laid the top run, Sofia the belly, and Jack took the forked tail. Omar took the fins. When he reached the pad, he found the fins already drawn with a dorsal fin label beside them, so he added no duplicates and waited for the body to reach them.

The trouble came at the tail. Hana's top run stopped short of the tail's upper corner, and Jack's fork was still far from finished. "Honest count, Hana," Jack said, and the team agreed that if the fork wasn't standing when time ran out, they would shorten it rather than push a half-built fork.

With the clock nearly gone, Omar checked the joint and found the top run ended with nothing ahead of it. The fork wasn't standing to meet it. Nobody added anything. "We push as it is," Omar said, and the team did. 60 of 60 dominoes fell from the start, though the tail never closed.

Everyone at the table called it a fish. The blind judges guessed a boat, a leaf, and a fish.
