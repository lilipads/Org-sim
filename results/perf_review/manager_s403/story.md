## In short

The team had one pad and a fish sketched in a hurry. Maya held the pad, and the plan kept shifting. Halfway through, she redrew the fish so everything grew from a short spine at the tail, but the new drawing never reached Tom. "The pad still looks like the old drawing to me," he wrote. Lines started colliding until Leo spoke up: his bottom line already ran past where Tom stood. Leo asked Tom to help with the long top body instead. Maya and Leo met near the nose, and Tom checked the joint. Maya called the outline connected too early. Leo spotted a pale diagonal in the tail, bridged it, and nudged a loose domino. Priya stayed clear and watched the nose. Then everyone went hands off. When time ran out, the fish fell in a single sweep: 179 of 196 dominoes.
