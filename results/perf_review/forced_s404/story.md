## In short

Sofia chose a fish facing left, a big body with a forked tail that would be easy to recognize. She gave each teammate a stroke to trace: Omar took the bottom body, Hana the upper tail lobe, and Jack the lower one. Halfway through, Hana spotted a pale join where the top line left the split. Sofia asked for it to be closed, then corrected herself: "the top line is actually connected." The real break turned up at the upper tail, where the outer curve went pale at the tip. Hana found the first domino sitting off the line and bridged the gap. Near the end, Jack cleared a stray stub off the tail tip, and Hana asked him to leave the curve alone. Sofia ran a full check from the nose, and everyone kept their hands off the body. In the end, 129 of 131 pieces fell, and most blind guesses named a fish.
