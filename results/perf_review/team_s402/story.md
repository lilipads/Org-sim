## In short

Sofia proposed a fish: a big oval body with a triangle tail. Hana drew it on the pad and split the work, giving Sofia the top line, herself the bottom, Omar the tail, and Jack backup for gaps.

The first snag was the middle, where Sofia's top line ran a hair short and went pale. Jack found it, then stepped off when Hana took over the join. Omar flagged a jammed domino and a sharp kink, and closed the too-wide start gap. Hana spotted a stray pale loop past the tail, and Sofia took it down. After a hairpin at the tail, Hana branched a gentle curve into the middle and cleared a jammed clump so the bridges could go in.

Sofia re-laid the shaky bottom line round to the nose. Then Hana called "All hands off," and everyone let it fall. 133 of 155 dominoes fell, and everyone said fish. Blind judges guessed fish too.
