## In short

Sofia set the plan: start at the nose, split lines around the body, and finish with a forked tail. "Fish works for me," Hana said, and the team divided the work. Sofia took the top line, Omar the bottom, and Jack and Hana the tail forks.

The first turning point came at the junction. Hana's inspection found the bottom line stopping short of the tail, and Jack spotted a domino sitting off to the side. Omar laid the line right up to the tail, then nudged the stray into place.

The second came with a pale line above the tail. Hana planned to take it down, but Sofia stepped in: "the upper tail fork going up-right is part of the fish, so please keep it." Omar confirmed the stray was gone and the fork was still standing.

After a final look from nose to tail, everyone went hands off. The timer ran down, the start domino was pushed, and the fish fell. The result: 99 of 99 dominoes fell, and the blind judges all guessed fish.
