# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.

- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Four teammates set out to build a fish against the clock. Leo sketched the plan: "a fish, a side view with a big tail fin, easy to recognize." He laid the start and lead-in, then handed out the rest: Maya the top of the body, Tom the belly, Priya the tail.

The first snag came on the belly, where Leo spotted a domino jammed too close to the next one. Tom went to fix that tight spot. Maya offered to carry the back half of the belly, and Leo kept the stretches apart so no one laid the same one.

The real test was the tail's bottom corner, where a gap sat between Maya's lower fin and Priya's edge. Priya checked it, said it looked closed, and asked everyone to keep hands off. "Everything standing connects back to the start," she wrote.

When the push came, all 123 dominoes fell. The judges guessed fish, a side-view outline with body, tail, and fin.
