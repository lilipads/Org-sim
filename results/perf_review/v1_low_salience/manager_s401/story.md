# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Four teammates had a short clock to build a fish out of dominoes. Priya had the pad, laid the start and the top line, and kept an eye on the joints. Maya took the bottom line, and Tom carried it out toward the tail. Leo took the tail, though his plan kept shifting. He first split the tail into halves, then admitted the pad had no separate strokes, and finally drew one back edge through a notch.

The first turning point came when Tom removed his own stray stub from the bottom line, and Priya chose to leave a harmless bit alone. The second came near the end. Priya nudged a kink at the lower tip, and Leo asked everyone to add nothing new before the push. Blind judges mostly saw a fish, though a few guessed tadpole or bird. The whole design fell: 113 of 113.
