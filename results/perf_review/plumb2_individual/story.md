## In short

Four teammates set out to build a fish, and their first problem was the plan. Sofia proposed the fish, and Hana sketched it on the pad with the body running from START and a forked tail at the far end. The numbering then went wrong, and Hana kept redrawing the pad while Jack asked for the body to be drawn first. Once the plan held, Jack laid the top line from START toward the nose, Omar ran the bottom edge, and Sofia held the fork until the top reached the junction. Hana checked the side-step by START and the joins as they went. Near the end, Omar's bottom edge fell short of the fork, so he used a connect rather than an extend. He was still placing it when time ran out. Every domino fell: 121 of 121.
