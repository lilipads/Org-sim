# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.

- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Hana sketched a fish facing left and split the work. Omar traced the belly, Sofia took the upper and lower tail, and Jack waited to close the back edge. Once Omar's belly reached the tail junction, Sofia's lines reached the tips. The top line stayed pale by the nose, though, so Omar checked the join and Hana bridged it. Stray lines kept turning up near the nose and inside the body. Sofia lifted them carefully, and Jack checked the tail notch. When Hana called for hands off, Sofia ran the final check and reported that the outline carried through, with only a few loose bits inside the head. "Hands off and let it fall!" she said. When the start domino was pushed, 110 of 116 fell, and the blind judges' guesses all named a fish.
