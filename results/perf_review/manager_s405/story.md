# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Twenty minutes, one fish. Leo picked it because "it reads clearly from the outline alone," and he started the body at the nose. Priya drew the sketch and put it on the pad so nobody had to wait. Tom took the bottom body line, Maya took the top tail fin and then the top fin, and Priya and Maya closed the back edge.

The turning point came near the end. Tom spotted "a little pale line of dominoes inside the body" that wasn't meant to be there and lifted it. Then Leo's fix at the bottom corner "didn't take," leaving a gap where the fin bent into the back edge. Leo stepped back, and Tom stepped in: "Fix didn't take, so I'm laying a proper join across that bottom corner."

When the start domino was pushed, 131 of 189 fell. Everyone called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed an ichthys symbol, a fish outline, and a stylized eel.
