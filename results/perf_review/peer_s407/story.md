# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

In twenty minutes, four teammates set out to build a fish from dominoes. Maya proposed it: "Let's do a fish, the outline is easy to read and the tail makes it obvious." Leo sketched the design, and the work split into four strokes. Tom took the top curve, Priya the bottom, Maya the upper tail, and Leo the lower tail.

The first real snag came at the tail joint. Tom's top curve reached it, but the line rising from the joint stopped short of the upper tail. "Fix wouldn't take on that gap," Tom said, so he bridged it with a connector. Priya nudged one loose domino into line. Later, Leo spotted pale strays above the joint, confirmed they weren't connected to anything, and lifted only the left cluster.

With five minutes left, Leo's lower tail was "lit end to end," and everyone went hands-off so nothing would get bumped before the push.

Result: 136 of 142 fell. Every blind judge guessed a fish.
