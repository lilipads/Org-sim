# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At this table, the team chose a fish. Leo sketched it and laid out the roles: "Maya, you take the bottom body line. Priya, you take the upper tail fin. Tom, you take the lower tail fin." He ran the top line and the start at the nose.

The turning point came at the upper fin. Priya noticed it was "still pale at the base," and Maya's first nudge didn't take. Leo said it still looked pale to him, so he studied the crossing while Priya moved on to the notch between the fins. Then Maya re-laid the fin: "it's dark all the way to the tip." Once the whole run read connected, the team agreed on "Hands off the tail now, everyone."

Priya called the final stretch: "Everything's connected, so hands off and let it fall." Tom added, "Here goes the fish!" The result: 122 of 122 fell.
