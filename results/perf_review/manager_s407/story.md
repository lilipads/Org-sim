# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya started with a plan: a fish with a forked tail. She sketched it, then split the strokes. "Tom, take the bottom curve. Priya, the tail. Leo, the closing edge." Tom laid the start stretch first, and Maya's top curve grew from it.

Trouble came when Leo spotted Priya's lower lobe running too flat, and she re-laid it. The fall stalled at the body. Tom closed the gap, and Priya suggested a domino nudged into the junction. After Tom said to take it down, Leo and Priya cleared a stray loop from the right.

With time running out, Maya bridged the body and it turned dark. Maya joined the lower lobe, Tom made the tail join, and Priya confirmed it ran dark to the tail.

Then Tom called it: "Hands off everyone, it looks like a fish." At the push, 131 of 190 fell. Everyone said fish. Blind judges were split, naming a fish, a swan, a bird, or a sprout.
