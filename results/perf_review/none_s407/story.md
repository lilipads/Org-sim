# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Four teammates had twenty minutes to build a fish out of dominoes, and it started with a plan. Maya sketched the outline and called out, "Let's do a fish!" Priya had drawn her own fish on a blank pad, but Maya said it was the same as hers, so they went with it. Maya laid the top line, Tom took the bottom, and Priya and Leo each took a tail piece.

The top line was the slow part. Tom carried it on from the head while Maya bridged the last gap into the junction. During inspection, a stray domino jammed at the crossing where the tail met the body. Maya lifted the loose ones out, then re-checked the chain from the start.

Then the team kept their hands off. "Hands off, here it comes!" Maya said. Tom added, "Here we go, good luck little fish!" The fish fell, and all 114 of 114 dominoes went down.
