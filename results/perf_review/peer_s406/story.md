## In short

Sofia wanted a fish: an oval body with a triangle tail, easy to read. She laid the top line from the nose while Omar ran the bottom line. Jack added a small fin branching off Sofia's top line. Hana was meant to take the tail, but when Omar picked it up, she said, "Omar, you keep the tail, I'll stay off it," and took on checking the joins.

The turning point came at the junction, where the tail met the body. The tail was still pale there, so Sofia bridged the gap, while Hana spotted a weak point where the fin came down to the body and worked to close it. Sofia's nudges changed nothing, so the team agreed to leave the fish alone. "Hands off till the push, fingers crossed!" Omar wrote.

When the start domino went, the whole fish fell: 119 of 119. The blind judges mostly saw a fish too, though one thought of a submarine.
