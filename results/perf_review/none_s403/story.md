# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team chose a fish. Maya drew the outline facing left and laid the top line, Tom took the bottom, and Priya and Leo claimed the tail. The junction became the trouble spot. Maya reported that her fix "didn't take," and the lower fin still wouldn't pick up. Leo checked the joins and pulled out a pale diagonal near Priya's hands. Then Priya spotted a line Tom was laying toward the bottom right and warned that it "isn't part of the fish." She lifted the stray part, and Tom answered, "You're right, Priya, my bad," and stopped. Hands came off the table, and everyone watched. In the end, 117 of 123 fell, and the team called it a fish.
