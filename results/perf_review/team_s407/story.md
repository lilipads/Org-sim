## In short

Tom sketched a fish for the pad, a body outline with a big tail fin so the judge couldn't miss it. Maya took the start at the nose, and Priya laid the belly line down to the tail. The snag came when Tom was already on the lower tail, so Priya switched to the back edge, the short line joining the two tail tips. Leo finished the upper tail and checked its joins. Maya carried the top line toward the tail junction, and the last gap needed help from Priya and Tom.

The turning point came when Maya checked the whole run: "by eye the whole run is connected." Tom and Priya agreed, and the table went hands off. "Hands off, everyone, and let's just watch the push." When the push came, the result was 127 of 127 fell. The blind judges guessed fish, though some saw a forked tail and others a looped ribbon.
