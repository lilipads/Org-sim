# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a side-view fish, nose pointing left, and split the work. Maya took the top curve, Tom the belly, and Priya the forked tail. "Each one begins where the last line ends," Leo explained, so every stroke would wait for the fall to reach it.

The first snag was the belly, which lagged behind the top curve. Tom asked Priya to take its back half from the tail end, but Maya ran the belly on to the tail herself, and Tom backed off so they wouldn't bump. Tom then spotted a kink near the middle and smoothed it out. Priya found a gap where the belly stopped short of the tail joint, but Maya replied, "I've got that gap," and Priya stepped aside.

The last turning point was the pale stray dominoes above the joint. Tom lifted them while Leo warned, "Everyone else, please don't touch the body now." With the chain connected from the start, the team went hands off and waited. The push brought down 137 of 137.
