# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a fish on the pad and split the work: Maya would take the belly, Tom the tail fin, and Priya the eye and any fixes. Maya's belly line ran up to the tail while Leo traced the top line toward the nose. Priya closed the nose joint, then turned to the fin, laying its lower edge.

The fin became the trouble spot. "It's not connected at the junction yet," Priya admitted, and her re-laid stroke still wouldn't catch. Tom took the lower edge and started a full redo, while Priya kept stepping in to bridge corners and close gaps nearby. "Redo's going down now," Tom wrote, and with minutes left Priya warned, "Don't pick those dominoes up again!"

When the start domino fell, 76 of 103 went with it. Everyone called the picture a fish, but the blind judges saw a leaf, a seed or bean, and a lemon-shaped loop, and only one mentioned a fish outline.
