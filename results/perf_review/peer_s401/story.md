# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At a table with twenty minutes on the clock, four teammates set out to build a fish. Leo sketched it facing left and split the work. Maya laid the bottom line, and when it reached the tail join, Tom started the tail off her line, since there was "no need to wait for Leo."

The top line ran short on time, so Priya offered to take the last stretch from the middle. Leo kept it, saying two of them on it would collide. When the upper lobe would not catch, Priya bridged to it and ran a fresh line, which curled short of the top tip. A piece of Tom's near the lobe got pulled.

With two minutes left, Leo froze the tail, then corrected himself: he had been "too confident." "Hands off and let the fish swim," Priya said. When the start domino fell, 149 of 188 went down, and blind judges guessed a fish.
