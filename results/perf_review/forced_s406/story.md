# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Sofia proposed a fish, since its forked tail would make it unmistakable. She laid the nose and top line, Omar took the bottom of the body, and Hana and Jack each set one half of the tail fin. The body came together quickly. "Top line's landed at the tail," Sofia called, and Omar's bottom line followed.

The fin was the hard part. Hana's top piece drifted out to the side and stayed pale, so Sofia took it over and laid it fresh from the pad. Hana had spotted a gap first, and Jack's bridge joined the top fin. Then a bump knocked dominoes off the fin's right edge, and Omar stood them back up: "Right edge is stood back up, Jack, you're clear."

With time nearly gone, Jack left a small stub inside the back edge alone: "A redo now could break something that works." Everyone kept their hands off. Each blind judge saw a fish in their guess, though they also named a leaf, a bird or a tadpole. The result: 153 of 157 fell.
