# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a fish on the pad and split the work: he took the top body, Maya the bottom, Tom the tail, and Priya the fins. Early on, Maya's bottom line stalled, so Maya handed it to Priya and moved to the bottom fin. Priya asked that the fins branch off the body lines so they would stay connected.

Trouble gathered near the end. Priya admitted she had called the bottom line joined too early, and the real break sat at the upper tail, where it leaves the body. Priya chose to re-lay the whole tail by hand, but her redo "seems to have gone down instead of up." Maya spotted the pale domino at the join, and Leo checked that everything standing was connected. With time nearly gone, the team agreed: "hands off for the push." Priya called out, "Let's watch it go!" Every domino fell, 157 of 157, and everyone who saw it called it a fish.
