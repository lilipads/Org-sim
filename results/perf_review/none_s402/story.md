## In short

Around a table, a team set out to build a fish from dominoes before time ran out. Hana sketched the body and took the lead-in and top line. Sofia laid the bottom arc, Omar took the tail, and Jack waited to add the eye and fins. Sofia caught the trouble early: the top line was pale at the split and not catching the fall. Hana fixed the join, but sharp turns kept surfacing. Sofia's inspection showed the top line still stopped short of the tail, and Omar's tail curled instead of forking. Jack spotted a jammed domino at the junction, and Omar re-laid the lower fork. As the clock wound down, Hana pulled stray pale pieces to close the gap. Then came the call: "Hands off, and let's see what falls." Judges guessed branches, lightning and rivers. The team had drawn a fish. In the end, 123 of 230 dominoes fell.
