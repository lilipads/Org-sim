## In short

Four teammates set out to build a fish facing left. Hana sketched the plan and split the work: she took the top of the body, Sofia the belly, and Omar and Jack the tail fin strokes. Hana's top line was set to meet Sofia's belly at the crossing.

The first turning point came when Sofia offered to take the lower fin. Jack, already well along it, asked her to leave it. Then Hana spotted the fin bending off the upper fin. She asked Jack to pause and relaid it straight down-right from the crossing, while Jack checked the upper fin.

With the fin set, Hana inspected the whole tail area, and everyone kept their hands off. Omar reported the fins connected to the start. When the push came, Hana posted, "Here we go."

Result: all 97 dominoes fell. The team said it was a fish, and blind judges guessed a fish with a forked tail.
