## In short

Sofia opened with a simple plan: a fish, sketched on the pad with a big forked tail so it would be obvious. She laid the top of the body from the nose, while Omar took the bottom line, Hana the upper tail fin, and Jack the lower tail. An early mix-up over the first stroke was sorted out quickly. Then Jack's bump knocked some lower-fin dominoes down, and he stood them back up, admitting, "that bump was me." Sofia nudged jammed joints, and Hana redid the upper tail, bridging the arc back to the notch. Omar fixed a wobbly piece by the lower notch. Near the end, Jack cleared the stray pale lines underneath while leaving the fin untouched. As time ran out, the team kept its hands off. Then the fish fell: 144 of 169 dominoes.
