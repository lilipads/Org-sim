# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team chose a fish, and Maya reached for the pad. Priya already had it, sketched and ready. "I've actually got the pad, so I've sketched the fish already," she said. She laid the top line and the start, then handed out the rest. Maya took the bottom body line, Tom the top tail stroke, and Leo the bottom tail stroke.

The first real pause came at the tail. Priya inspected the joins, found one domino sitting a touch off line, and nudged it. "Please keep hands off the tail until I say it's clean," she asked, and the others agreed. Leo and Tom confirmed their strokes ran through, and Maya checked the nose joint.

When the facilitator pushed the start, all 98 dominoes fell. Everyone had called it a fish. The blind judges' guesses ranged from fish outline to ichthys symbol to leaf.
