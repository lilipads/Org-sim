## In short

The fish began as a plan. Priya sketched the outline, Leo took the top line, and Tom took the bottom edge. Maya claimed the tail fin, and Priya took the bottom tail tip. For most of the round, the table moved in step. Leo called out when the top line reached the middle, and Maya waited at the fork.

The turning point came at the bottom join. Tom asked everyone to keep off it until he called it in, and Leo replied, "I'm not touching the bottom join." Then Leo noticed the top line had no start domino at its end, so he laid it himself while Priya finished her tip and waited.

Tom kept laying as the clock wound down, still a few dominoes short of the fork. "Good luck, Tom," Leo wrote. Then the final push came, and only a single domino fell. Everyone had called it a fish, but the blind judges saw only a speck on a blank page.
