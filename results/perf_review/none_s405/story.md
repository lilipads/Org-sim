# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a fish with its nose at the start and split the work: the top of the body for himself, the bottom for Maya, the tail for Tom, and the fin and eye for Priya. Early on, the start only knocked over a few pieces, so something between it and the body was broken. Priya fixed the loose pieces beside it.

Later, the top body was still pale at the tail junction, and Priya hooked it into the dark line. Maya spotted the real problem: "I think the top body runs nose to tail, so the break is at the nose." She bridged the nose while Priya cleared stray loops to free up dominoes. Tom then found the top body dropped out at the tail junction, fixed it, and bridged it again.

With time nearly up, Priya called out, "Hands off, everyone, let's let it ride. Fingers crossed, fish!"

Result: 100 of 249 fell. Everyone said fish, and blind judges guessed hooks, tadpoles, swans and snakes.
