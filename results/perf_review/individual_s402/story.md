*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.

- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Hana chose a fish facing left with a big forked tail and laid out the plan. She drew the top body line, Sofia took the bottom, Omar the top tail fin, and Jack the bottom tail fin. Sofia soon found a small gap where her line met the tail and bridged it.

The notch between the fins proved the hardest spot. Again and again it went off the line, and Hana kept stopping to fix it while asking everyone to stay clear. Omar hit a tip break that was too big to nudge, so he re-laid the whole top fin. Sofia tied the pale fin base into the body, and Hana fixed a jammed domino at the tail joint.

With time nearly gone, the team kept their hands off. "Hands off, everyone. Here goes!" Hana called. In the end, 171 of 250 fell. The players all saw a fish, and the blind judges guessed a fish, a fishing lure, or a ribbon bow.
