*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

## In short

**Fish on the Floor**

Priya had a fish in mind: side-on, facing left, with a V tail. She laid the start and top of the body. Maya took the belly, Tom the upper tail, and Leo the lower.

Leo caught the top line laid nose to tail, facing the wrong way. He redid it from the joint out to the nose.

Then the belly and lower tail turned pale. Priya laid both again, tail first, while Maya stayed clear. Tom found a jammed domino at the bend by the head, and Leo rebuilt the whole top line.

With time nearly gone, Maya carried the belly's last stretch to the nose while Priya bridged the joint. Then Priya reported, "From my picture the whole fish is dark now," from head to tail.

Result: 105 of 143 fell. Everyone said fish, and the blind judges guessed fish, ichthys symbol, or leaf shape.
