# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Jack opened with a big fish, sketching it on the pad and laying the top of the body. Sofia swept the belly round toward the tail. When Omar got slow, Sofia took the tail herself, and Omar bridged the top line over to meet it. Hana added the top fin and skipped the eye, since it would be "one more joint that could break." The turning point came at the tail, where Sofia found a small kink and nudged it into line. Then the team went hands off and watched for bumps. When the start domino was pushed, all 139 of 139 fell. Judges guessed fish, shark, whale, dolphin, and bird, but the players all saw one thing: a fish.
