## In short

Maya grabbed the pad first, and the team settled on a fish: a nose at the front, a top line and a belly line meeting at a forked tail. Priya spotted a break near the nose and bridged it: "I'm closing that gap now." Leo lifted the upper fork and laid it fresh when its joint stayed pale. Stray pale lines ran down from the nose, and Leo asked, "that pale line running down from the nose isn't in our sketch, is it?" Tom and Maya lifted them out, keeping off the belly line where it crossed.

With the nose dark, Tom walked the run and found it carried through both bodies and both tails. Then Leo corrected the record: he had only checked the tail joint, not fixed it. Hands off, the team watched the fish go. 100 of 111 dominoes fell, and blind judges saw a fish.
