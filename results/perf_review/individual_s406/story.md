## In short

The team had a timed build to make a fish out of dominoes. Sofia drew the plan: start at the tail, run one line down the middle, then split around the body and meet again at the nose. Hana took the tail, Omar the top of the body, and Jack the bottom.

The neck became the trouble spot. Sofia spotted a gap where the tail line turned toward the body, and the body line stayed pale. Hana put in a bridge of her own, then backed off when Sofia asked her to hold off. Sofia cleared the strays heading down-right, and Hana took down more below the neck.

Late on, Omar's lone bridge past the neck was jamming the start of the body, and Jack asked him to pull it out. Sofia re-laid the main line instead, and Omar dropped a bridge at the nose.

When time ran out, everyone stepped back. Sofia's last nudge was at the start of the bottom line. The fish fell 91 of 224 dominoes. All four teammates called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed a river, a signature, or a trail.
