## In short

**The Fish That Fell**

Hana started the team on a fish. She sketched the outline and laid the top of the body from the nose, while Omar took the bottom and Sofia and Jack took the tail and gill line. The pad was blank for a moment, then the drawing appeared. Hana laid the upper tail herself, and Sofia took the lower tail once Omar's bottom line reached the back. Jack's edge drifted, so Hana asked him to curve it back left. When the clock ran low, Sofia found crowded spots at the corner but judged the tail "just crowded, not broken." She decided not to redo it, since that would only risk breaking something with the time left. Hana checked from the start, nudged a high point on the bottom line, and the team kept their hands off the table. When the facilitator pushed the start, all 146 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a fish, while the blind judges guessed a bird, a swan, or a rope loop.
