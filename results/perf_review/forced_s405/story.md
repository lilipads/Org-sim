# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Four teammates had twenty minutes to domino a fish facing left, with a forked tail on the right. Leo opened with the plan: "Let's do a fish, facing left, with a big forked tail on the right." Priya laid the top line from the nose, Maya took the bottom, and Tom worked the tail. Leo took the fin and eye and walked the chain for gaps.

The first scare came when the start knocked over only a handful. Maya rebuilt the belly joint, but Priya found it ending beside the top line at the nose. She bridged it. Tom fixed the lower fork handoff, and Leo's trace showed the start reaching everything standing. A stray curl near the nose threatened the outline until Maya pulled it back. "Curl's cleared," she reported, and the team kept its hands off.

When time ran out, the push sent every piece down: 161 of 161 fell, and all four saw a fish.
