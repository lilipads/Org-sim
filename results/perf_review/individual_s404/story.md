# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Jack opened the pad with a plan: "Let's do a fish, the tail fin makes it easy to recognize." He sketched the back line, and Omar laid the belly, which reached the tail joint with time to spare. Hana claimed the forked tail while Sofia took the lower lobe. Then the first snag appeared. Hana noticed Jack's back line looked pale near the mouth, and a small gap there kept the chain from lighting up. Jack patched the joint, but the pale stretch stayed. Hana found the whole upper lobe was pale, not just the notch, and bridged the tail joint instead. Sofia and Omar crowded the mouth, each asking for hands off, while Jack waited so they wouldn't bump. With moments left, Jack made the connect himself, and Omar finished it: "Connect's down at the mouth. Hands off everyone, let it ride!"

When time ran out, 106 of 238 fell. Everyone said fish. The blind judges saw a horse, a serpent, a giraffe's neck, and a long-necked bird.
