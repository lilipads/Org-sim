## In short

The team chose a fish. Sofia planned the body and assigned the bottom to Omar, the upper tail to Hana, and the lower tail to Jack. Hana laid the nose and start, and Jack finished the upper fin after a quick mix-up: "change of plan, sorry for the confusion." Hana took the lower fin.

Sofia's top line reached the tail first. Omar's bottom was still under halfway, so Sofia laid the back half from the tail toward him. "Deal!" Omar replied, and the lines met in the middle.

The biggest catch came when Hana noticed the start domino wasn't joined to the nose, and bridged it. Hana then asked Sofia to stop building a loop in the middle that connected to nothing. Later, Omar spotted a break at the start of the upper fin, and Sofia lifted a jammed domino.

Omar counted down, "Three, two, one... push it!" The result: 151 of 205 fell. Everyone called it a fish.
