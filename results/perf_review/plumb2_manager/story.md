# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

**Fish, Fall Forward**

Maya proposed a fish: a big, simple body with a tail fin on the left. Priya took the pad, laid the top edge from tail to nose, and handed out strokes. Tom took the tail, Leo the little top fin, and Maya the bottom edge. Tom started the tail from the tip, which would have run the fall backwards. Priya caught it: "The fall comes in from the arms." Tom lifted the piece and laid it again from the top arm out to the tip. Leo confirmed the top fin's left foot was joined. Near the end, Maya spotted a domino sitting off the line at her bottom edge and nudged it straight, and Leo started the lower arm just past it. When the start domino fell, all 107 went down. Everyone called it a fish. The blind judges guessed birds and swans.
