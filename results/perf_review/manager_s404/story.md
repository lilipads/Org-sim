## In short

The team of Sofia, Omar, Hana and Jack chose a fish facing left. Sofia laid the nose and top curve, Omar took the bottom curve, and Hana claimed the tail. Jack planned to add the upper fin and an eye.

The turning point came at the nose joint. The top curve stayed pale, and Jack spotted it. Sofia suspected the curve left the nose at too sharp an angle, and Hana confirmed it by bridging the gap. Later, a stray line rose from the crossing like a spike. Hana spotted it and took it down, and the team agreed the little stubs could stay.

With hands off, everyone waited. "Here we go, team," Jack wrote. When the start domino was pushed, 111 of 115 dominoes fell. The blind judges all guessed fish-like shapes. "Great fish, everyone!" Sofia said.
