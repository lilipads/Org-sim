## In short

Jack chose a fish with a big body and a forked tail, sketched it on the pad, and handed out the strokes: Hana the top of the body, Jack the belly, Sofia the upper tail lobe, and Omar the lower lobe. The top line ran fast, but trouble surfaced at the nose, where the top line and belly split. Sofia spotted pale joints there. Hana cleared an overshooting dark run and linked it into the top line. Jack then found weak spots at the tail, and Omar re-laid the lower lobe from the pad. Sofia's nose link kept failing, "too crowded to link there," so she cleared stray dominoes to make room. In the final minutes Jack's fork lit, both lobes went dark, and Sofia and Hana worked to finish the nose link. Jack summed up: "Great teamwork, everyone!" In the end, 142 of 250 dominoes fell. Every player called it a fish, while the blind judges guessed a fish, a bird, or a ribbon.
