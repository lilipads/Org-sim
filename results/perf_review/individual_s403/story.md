# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

## In short

At a table of four, Tom sketched a fish and handed out its parts: the top line to himself, the belly to Maya, and the tail forks to Priya and Leo. The early work ran smoothly, with Leo promising to take his fork "as soon as it's on the pad."

The first turning point came at the tail crossing. Maya patched tight spots there, but Leo spotted that the whole lower fork still looked pale. Tom stepped back: "Leo, the joint's yours, I'll stay off it." Leo found a domino sitting off to the side, nudged it, and then relaid the entire fork while Maya kept clear.

With minutes left, Maya cleared the notch and upper tail, while Leo hunted the last break at the far end of the lower tail. A stray jammed there came out, and he closed the tip. Tom checked from the start and called the fish dark through the tail. Then everyone kept their hands off and let it fall.

Result: 151 of 182 fell. Every player called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed fish too.
