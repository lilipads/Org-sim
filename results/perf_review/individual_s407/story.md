# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Maya, Leo, Priya and Tom chose a fish with a forked tail. Maya took the top line, Leo the bottom, Priya the tail strokes, and Tom the edge across the back of the tail. Leo's bottom line stopped short of the join, so he carried it on to the fork as Maya brought her top line in. Priya laid the lower tail down to its tip.

Then came a near collision. Priya started the back edge from the bottom, but Tom was already coming down from the top: "Don't lay it from the bottom or we'll double up."

The fork caused the most trouble. Leo fixed a small break there, but the upper tail wouldn't catch until Priya and Tom bridged the gap. Then Maya's bump knocked the fork and tail down. She stood them back up while the others kept clear.

In the end, the upper tail went dark and connected. 136 of 182 dominoes fell, and everyone said it was a fish.
