# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At a table of four, Leo proposed a side-on fish, nose on the left and tail on the right, and split the work. He took the start and top line, Maya the belly, Tom the tail, and Priya the eye and a fin on top. Priya had already sketched the design, so the plan fit it neatly. Priya nudged a kink out of the fin, then reported that "everything else still carries through from the start."

The trouble came at the tail. Tom said he would carry on round the whole tail himself. When Priya offered to take the lower join, he called the pale bit "just a spare" and declined. Maya then noticed the fin still looked pale and worked on its base. As Leo pointed out the last gap past the fin, Maya closed it.

With time nearly gone, the team agreed: "Hands off, let it fall!" All 138 dominoes fell. Every player called it a fish, and every blind judge's top guess was a fish.
