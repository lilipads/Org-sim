## In short

A team set out to topple a domino fish within a short time limit. Leo grabbed the pad and sketched the plan, and Maya agreed: "going with your sketch." Leo took the top arc, Tom the bottom line, and Priya the tail fin, while Maya kept watch over the joints.

The first snag came at the tail. Tom spotted a gap at the bottom corner and nudged it closed. Near the end, the crossing became the worry. Tom announced a fix, then reversed course: "I'm not touching it after all." Leo inspected the spot, nudged it, found the fix hadn't taken, and bridged the top arc into the crossing with a few more dominoes.

When time ran out, the fish was dark except for a few pale spares. Its outline was dark enough to read as a fish to the team. The result: 116 of 126 dominoes fell. The blind judges saw loop-tails, figure-eights, and ribbons.
