# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo suggested a fish, since its outline is easy to read and the tail gives it away. Maya sketched it and split the work: Tom took the bottom line, Priya the tail, and Leo the joint where the body met the tail. The joint became the trouble spot. Leo bridged it while the lower arm stayed pale, and Tom's bump knocked over several pieces there. Leo and Tom stood them back up. Maya removed a stray curve creeping toward the top-right corner, and Priya checked that the top arm and closing edge held. With time nearly gone, Leo fixed the last breaks near the tip while everyone else kept their hands off. In the end, 110 of 194 dominoes fell. Everyone said it was a fish, and Maya had already reasoned it through: "Even if it misses, the body and top arm should still read as a fish."
