# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a fish and split the work: he took the top line from the nose, Maya the bottom, Tom the tail, and Priya the eye and fin. Maya had already queued her bottom line, so Priya built the fin and then turned to help Tom. A pale gap right after the fin needed bridging, and Priya closed it while Leo checked the same stretch. The tail joint became the real trouble. Priya found a domino sitting off to the side, and Tom was already bridging that joint. Tom said, "Hands off, everyone!" Priya replied, "Let's let the fish run!" The team left the chain alone and let it fall. In the end, 121 of 177 fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed a bird, a boat, a leaf, and sometimes a fish.
