# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

## In short

Sofia proposed a fish, sketched it on the pad, and set the start at the nose, with the top line running back from it. Omar took the bottom line, Jack the forked tail, and Hana the eye. Lines were handed off with a steady "Sounds good," from one player to the next.

The first snag came when Hana's eye spur branched off the middle of the body instead of the head. She lifted it, laid it again, and held the ring until the spur was confirmed.

The back joint caused the longest worry. Sofia kept nudging gaps closed, and Jack, asked to take the upper flagged domino, went gently while Sofia handled the lower one. Everyone kept their hands off the joint while the clock ran down.

Hana checked the nose join, and then reported that the chain ran through everything standing. When the start was pushed, all 111 of 111 fell, and the blind judges guessed fish.
