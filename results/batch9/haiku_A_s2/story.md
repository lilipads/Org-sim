# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya, Priya, Tom and Leo chose a fish within minutes. Maya drew the body and forked tail and laid the top line from the nose, while Priya took the bottom line so they wouldn't share a stroke. Tom built the tail fins, and Leo took the nose join, promising to fix any gaps there.

The nose kept refusing to close. Leo bridged it again and again, and Priya held her last dominoes until it read joined. Maya finished the tail, and Tom laid the fins. Then Tom looked from his end and found the snag: "the gap is at the bend just below the nose, where the next domino sits off to the side of its line."

Maya fixed that bend, and Tom nudged the jammed domino past START into line. Leo was still on the connect when time ran out.

Result: 123 of 168 fell. Everyone called it a fish, though the blind judges also guessed a leaf, a boat, and a lemon or almond shape.
