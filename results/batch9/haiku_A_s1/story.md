# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Maya picked a fish, and Priya sketched it facing left with its nose at the start spot. Maya took the forked tail, Priya traced the body, and Tom watched the joins along the line. Then Leo became the inspector. He found a gap above the start, and his first bridge "didn't take," so he asked everyone to keep their hands off that stretch. Nearly the whole top edge had to be retraced from the start, while Priya nudged the out-of-line domino at the right tip and Maya waited to lay the eye. Leo finally confirmed the start carried all the way to the fork and called the area clear. Maya traced the eye, Leo gave the whole picture a final inspection, and he called, "Let's let it go." All 105 of 105 fell. The team said fish, and the judges guessed everything from fish facing left to a bird's head in profile.
