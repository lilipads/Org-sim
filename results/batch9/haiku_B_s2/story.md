# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Sofia, Hana, Omar, and Jack had a short clock to build a fish out of dominoes. Sofia sketched the outline and took the tail fin, announcing that "a fish with a tail fin reads clearly." Hana traced the top edge from the tail tip to the nose, while Omar laid the bottom line and held it at the tail. Jack stayed off the fin and offered to check the joins.

The first turning point came when Sofia flagged a bend under the start, where the fin meets the body, as a spot where two dominoes wouldn't fit. Hana inspected it carefully and found no real break. "I'd just be adding joints that could break," she said, so she bridged nothing and kept her hands off.

The second came near the end. Sofia spotted a stray domino sitting off to the side by the tail. Hana urged Omar to shout "even if it's not perfect," and the team waited for his signal.

The clock ran out with Omar still bringing the last dominoes toward the tail. When the start was pushed, every connected piece fell: 115 of 115.
