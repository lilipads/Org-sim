## In short

Jack proposed a fish: a big oval body and a triangle tail, easy to recognise. Sofia sketched it on the pad, and Hana caught a flaw. The start line sat right at the head, so the pad was redrawn with the start line leading. Sofia built a nose bridge, found it off target, and took it down. Her next try ran back along the start line and up into the top curve, which finally went dark. Hana bridged a gap before the tail point, while Omar traced the lower tail's jam to the crossing. Sofia cleared it and rebuilt the joint. In the final minutes, Jack and Hana joined the back edge, and Omar kept watch. Jack called, "Go fish!" as time ran out. Result: 93 of 223 fell. Blind judges guessed snail, bird, and teapot. The team still saw a fish.
