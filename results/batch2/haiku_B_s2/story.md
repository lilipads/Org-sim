# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Sofia picked a fish, a long body running from tail to nose. Hana set the plan, but Jack spotted a snag: a fall only runs one way, so a start at the body-tail join would leave the tail unable to fall backward. "Could the start go at the tail tip," he asked, and the team moved it there.

The old start had left a run of dominoes beside the new top edge. Hana cleared it, along with loose dominoes nearby. Sofia bridged a gap at the tip, and Omar laid the bottom edge and closed its nose end when Sofia's top edge fell short.

With time nearly gone, Hana inspected the start joint. The line bent too sharply just past it, and one domino was jammed against the next. Sofia eased the bend, but the top edge never reached the nose in time, and Jack's tail arms were still waiting.

When the start was pushed, 66 of 98 dominoes fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed swan, hook, wave, and bird.
