*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Sofia grabbed the pad and sketched a snake, one long wiggly line from the start. Hana agreed and asked for the head to be stroked first so the fall would run head to tail. Omar offered for the head circle, but Jack was already five dominoes into it, so Omar took the tail. Sofia took on the restand of the middle bump.

The turning point came near the end. Sofia admitted, "my hands were empty, so I hadn't actually started the restand." She started for real, but the jam just past the middle stayed crowded, and she kept saying it wasn't clear yet. Hana and Jack agreed to meet at the leftmost circle domino, while Omar fixed a joint at the base of his tail.

When the push came, 73 of 76 fell. Everyone called it a snake, but the blind judges guessed a lasso, a tadpole, or a whip.
