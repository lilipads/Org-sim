# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Priya opened with a plan: a fish, nose right, with the tail as the start. Tom and Leo agreed. Priya traced the top line, Leo took the bottom, and Tom took the belly, branching off just past the tail. Maya claimed the nose join.

The turning point came at the tail. Two branches left the top close together, so Priya asked Leo what he had told Tom. Leo explained, and Tom later promised, "Leaving that gap clear." Priya kept her hands off the table so the others had room.

As time ran short, each builder checked their own section. Leo confirmed his bottom met the nose. Tom confirmed the belly hadn't changed. Maya ran a final inspect and reported the whole fish connected from tail to nose.

The push carried through the whole chain: 58 of 58 fell. Everyone at the table said fish, while the blind judges guessed a leaf, an eye, and a fish.
