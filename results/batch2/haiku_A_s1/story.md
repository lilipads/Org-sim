# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish. Maya sketched the outline on the pad so everyone built the same thing. Priya took the tail and the start, Tom traced the bottom edge, Leo readied the top fin, and Maya added the little tail fin.

Early on, Maya spotted a gap where Priya's pale stretch met the dark run, and Priya promised to close it. Even after repeated inspections, it stayed open. Priya kept saying she was closing it, then admitted, "I've said 'closing it' too many times without checking."

Leo placed his fin early, then admitted it wasn't connected until the middle join closed. Maya lifted a jammed tail tip and re-laid it. Tom checked the back toward the middle and found it joined up to the bend, with the pale stretch only past Priya's gap.

When the push came, 63 of 100 fell. Everyone called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed everything from a tadpole to a vine.
