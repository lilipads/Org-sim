# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team chose a fish within minutes. Maya sketched it on the pad and split the work. Priya built the nose stem, then the lower tail. Tom traced the top curve, Leo took the bottom, and Maya set the top tail, after adding a new start domino just left of the nose. Trouble came at the crossing, where the tail lines met. Priya spotted a domino sitting off to the side, and Tom nudged it into line. Afterward, Tom confirmed that the pale extras were not connected and would simply stand. "Hands off, everyone," Priya said, and the team kept their distance. Tom joked about an abstract fish, and Maya replied, "it's a fish, Tom. Fins and all." When the push came, 100 of 114 dominoes fell. The blind judges saw a fish too, though some guessed a leaf, a bird, or a seed.
