# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team chose a fish, Tom's idea: a plain body and a triangle tail, "hard to get wrong." Maya ran the lead-in line. When Tom's top curve started too early, she asked him to "pull those back and restart it from there," and he hung it off the end of her line. Priya traced the bottom curve, and Leo took the tail.

The turning point came when Maya's start kept missing her line. It sat low, and she bridged the gaps one at a time until the top curve linked in properly. Tom spotted a gap at the tail junction, then found none after all. Maya called for hands off, and as time ran out, Priya said, "it's all connected," and let the fish swim.

Result: 125 of 126 fell. The team saw a fish, and the blind judges guessed one too, though some said a bird or a knot.
