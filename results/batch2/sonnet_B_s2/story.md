## In short

Four teammates set out to topple a fish in twenty minutes. Omar suggested the idea, and Jack sketched it on the pad and laid the lead-in. Omar took the top of the body, Sofia the bottom, and Hana the tail, while Jack took the top of the tail.

The first turning point came when Hana noticed a line heading toward the bottom right that wasn't on the sketch. Jack admitted, "that loop was my slip," and Sofia and Hana cleared it. The second came when Hana began taking down Sofia's diagonal, which was actually the tail's bottom edge. Sofia pleaded for it to stop, and Hana replied, "Sorry Sofia, my mistake, I misread it."

Omar fixed a pale join at the split, and a jammed domino at the point was cleared. As time ran short, the team called out "keep off it" and agreed on hands off until the push. The fish fell, with 86 of 194 dominoes toppling. Blind judges guessed a river, a tree branch, or a rope, but the team saw a fish.
