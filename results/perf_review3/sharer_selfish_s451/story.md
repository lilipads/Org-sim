## In short

At this table, the team set out to build a fish. Maya proposed the idea, and Priya sketched it on the pad: a pair of lines starting at the nose, curving around the body, and flaring into a tail. Tom took the top line, Leo the bottom, and Maya the tail end.

The first turning point came at the tail. Maya asked for the "pointy corners" to be rounded off, which Priya tried, but the pad kept showing the old notch. Maya kept pushing until the tail became "a soft flare" with "no doubling back."

The second came at the nose. Maya noticed the top line was pale, so it wasn't connected there, and she bridged the gap. Priya's first by-eye check was wrong, so she checked again and confirmed the whole run. With everyone's hands off, the push came, and 127 of 127 fell. The judges guessed a fish.
