# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Leo sketched a big side-on fish with a forked tail and split the work. Maya laid the start and traced the top line and top lobe. Priya took the belly and lower lobe, while Tom kept the bin stocked and stood ready to help with the tail.

The turning point came at the tail neck. Leo first wanted to split the lower lobe into its own stroke, then scrapped the idea: "Just carry on along the belly and out around the lower lobe as planned, and I won't trace anything there, so nothing gets laid twice." Priya finished the lobe alone. Maya stayed clear of the neck, and Leo nudged a crowded domino into line.

Priya's close look found that the whole tail carried through from the start. Tom walked the line for bumps and found nothing down. Then came the push. Almost every domino fell, and the blind judges mostly guessed fish, though some guessed a leaf or seed.
