## In short

Hana opened with a plan: a fish, drawn from nose to tail, with a V for the fin. Sofia took the top curve, Omar the bottom, and Jack the tail. Early on, the bottom line curled into a loop by the nose, and Sofia and Hana both flagged it. Omar pulled it out and redid it. Jack's upper fin curled too, and he rebuilt it as a straight diagonal.

As the clock ran down, the work turned into hunting for breaks. Hana checked the bottom curve, and Omar spotted a domino sitting off to the side, but the nudge changed nothing. Omar found the start run stopped short of the bottom curve and bridged the gap. Sofia fixed a break at the tail join, while Omar kept working the nose joint. Everyone said fish. Blind judges guessed a swoosh, a bird head, or a swan's neck. In the end, 64 of 222 fell.
