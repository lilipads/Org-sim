## In short

The team set out to topple a fish before time ran out. Priya drew the outline, and Leo claimed the tail as the start, with both fins meeting at a junction. Tom carried the bottom edge, while Maya saved the eye for last.

The trouble gathered at that junction. Leo joined the lower fin, but the top edge never hooked to the tail. "I missed that earlier, which is on me," he admitted. As the clock wound down, Priya fixed the jammed and off-line spots near the nose and confirmed the tip was joined. Leo and Maya agreed to let the body go and leave the eye out.

When the start domino was pushed, 71 of 188 fell. Blind judges guessed a curved arc, a bowl, or a smile, and the chain stopped at a loose end on the nose.
