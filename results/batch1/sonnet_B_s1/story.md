# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Jack opened with a plan: "how about a fish?" He sketched the body and split the work. Sofia took the top curve, Omar the bottom, and Hana and Jack shared the two tail lines.

The first turning point came when Sofia's early curl got in the way. She cleared it, but her top curve stopped short of the join. Hana offered to lay it, and Sofia asked everyone to hold off while she extended it from the end. Later, Hana spotted Jack's lower tail looping back and extended it toward the bottom right.

With five minutes left, the top curve reached the join. Sofia cleared her old curl and left only a harmless stub. Omar checked the whole route, and everyone kept their hands off. When the start domino was pushed, 110 of 129 fell. The team had called it a fish, though the blind judges guessed fish, bird, or leaf.
