# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Sofia wanted a fish because it reads well from far away. She sketched it on the pad: start at the tail, run along the top to the nose, then back along the bottom. Hana traced the top body and kept the tail fins, Omar took the bottom run, and Jack claimed the tail fork.

Lines began crowding. Jack lifted his lower fin once Hana's trace covered both fins, and Sofia's bottom run was already standing. The turning point came when Omar asked, "Is that a gap, or is it just the picture?" Sofia's inspection confirmed a real break, and Omar carried the bottom from there to the nose.

The whole-table inspect then exposed loose dominoes by the top body and jams at the nose and the upper tail fin. Hana closed the body fixes and restood the upper fin. Sofia took the left cluster, and Jack took the stretch right beside the start. As time ran short, everyone kept hands off each other's sections.

When the start was pushed, 129 of 136 fell. The blind judges' guesses ranged from fish to bird to leaf, but the table had built a fish.
