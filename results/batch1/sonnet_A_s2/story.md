# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Maya proposed a fish because it was "Easy to read," and sketched the plan: a top curve for Priya, a bottom curve for Tom, and a tail for Leo. Early on, Priya's top line curled back the wrong way. Maya pulled out the hook, and Priya carried on from the stub, sweeping up and over to the right.

The real turning point came at the tail joint. Tom carried the bottom line on to meet the top, but Maya's check found the first dominoes at the joint sitting off to the side. Tom bridged the last pair into place. Leo built the vertical, Priya laid the lower fin, and Tom bridged the top fin into the vertical.

With time nearly gone, Maya took over the top bridge while Leo spotted a pale top and a jammed spot below the bend. Maya and Tom closed those gaps. Then Maya called it: "Hands off everyone, no more nudging. Let's just let it go." In the end, 118 of 141 dominoes fell, and everyone agreed it was a fish.
