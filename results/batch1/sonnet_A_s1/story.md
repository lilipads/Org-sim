# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team agreed on a fish almost at once. Maya sketched it on the pad and split the work: Tom would trace the top curve, Priya the bottom, and Leo the tail. Tom's top line soon curled into a little circle near the nose, and Maya asked him to stop. He pulled it out and redrew it. Then Leo's tail closed into a ring, and Priya spotted it: "that tail end is a closed ring, not two lines." Leo agreed, and the ring came out. Maya found a gap just after the nose and closed it, while Tom and Leo both worked on the nose join. Near the end, the team cleared a cluster under the tail joint. Priya laid the lower tail, but it stayed pale at the joint, and she tried one last bridge as time ran out. Everyone called it a fish, and the blind judges guessed balloons, lassos, and spoons. When the start domino was pushed, 86 of 114 fell.
