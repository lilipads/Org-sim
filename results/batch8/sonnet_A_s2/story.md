# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish. Maya sketched the outline on the pad and split the work: Tom took the bottom of the body, Maya the top, and Priya and Leo the tail. The tail refused to lay out, so Leo drew the top diagonal as its own stroke. The turning point came when Priya looked at the tail joint: "The top line ends right at the joint pointing up and out with nothing ahead of it, and there's one domino jammed too close just beside it." Maya finished the top, Tom confirmed the body reached the joint, and Leo gave the tail one last look. After that, everyone kept their hands off. When the push came, it ran from the nose to both tail tips. "Go fish, go!" Maya called. The result: 96 of 96 fell, and the team agreed it read as a fish.
