# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team set out to draw a fish in dominoes: side view, nose to the right, forked tail on the left. Sofia laid the body from the tail joint, running the top edge toward the nose, while Hana took the bottom edge. Jack splayed the forked tail's lobes, and Omar hooked his dorsal fin onto the top line where it branched.

The turning point came at the top-lobe joint. Omar's inspect showed the top edge and his fin floating, cut off where the top lobe met the body. Sofia kept trying to bridge the gap, but her attempts placed nothing, and the others kept their hands off. Near the end, Hana wrote, "Sofia, please stop retrying that connect."

When time ran out, 46 of 116 dominoes fell. The bottom edge, tail and belly stayed connected to the start, but the upper lobes floated free. Everyone agreed it was a fish, though the blind judges guessed everything from a bird to a wave.
