# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

The team set out to build a fish before time ran out. Sofia sketched the plan, a nose, a body split into top and bottom lines, and a big forked tail, then handed out the jobs. When Jack pointed out that the pad's stroke labels didn't match her numbers, Sofia owned the mix-up with a quick "my bad," and the team adjusted. Hana laid the bottom body line while Sofia built the top. Omar became the floater, keeping clear of the others and patching gaps. The turning point came when Jack asked Omar to take the bottom half of the fin so they wouldn't stack on the same stroke. Later, Hana noticed the fin looked "looped rather than a clean triangle," but the team chose not to rip anything up. As the timer ran down, the watchword became "hands off." When the start domino was pushed, 136 of 136 fell, and everyone called it a fish.
