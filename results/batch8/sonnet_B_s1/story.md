*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Hana proposed a side-on fish with a big forked tail. Sofia sketched it on the pad and split the strokes: Hana the top line, Sofia the belly, Omar the upper tail, Jack the lower tail fork. Jack's fork came out as a loop and looked pale, so Hana warned it wouldn't fall. Jack retraced it while the others stayed clear. Sofia then lifted the stray stub near her hands, the last piece that would have been cut off. Jack told everyone, "Once the stub's gone, nobody touch the tail so we don't bump anything!" When time ran out, all 114 dominoes fell. Blind judges saw a fish with a forked tail, though some guessed a looped ribbon or a leaf.
