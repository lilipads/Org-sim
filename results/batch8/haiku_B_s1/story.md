## In short

Sofia sketched a fish on the pad: a V-shaped tail, a body, and a top edge running to the nose. Hana took the tail fin, Jack the dorsal fin, and Omar the eye. Everyone waited for Sofia's word before tracing off her lines.

Then the clock ran down. Omar checked the V join and reported it "connected back to the start," but Hana spotted the real gap: "nothing's standing between START and the V." No start domino had been laid. Sofia agreed, placed it, and traced toward the V while Jack checked the fin. He found the fin connected, and the only break was the open gap at the start. Omar kept bridging the eye's front gap as Sofia ran the top edge toward the nose.

When time was up, 39 of 86 dominoes fell. All four teammates said fish. The judges guessed a wave, a snake, a roller coaster, or a cursive letter.
