## In short

The team set out to build a fish from dominoes. Maya sketched the body, and Tom took the start. Leo spotted a snag: the tail fins ran against the direction of the fall. Maya moved the start to the top tail tip, so the fall ran through the tail point to the nose.

The lower arm became the trouble spot. Priya said it began at the top-left dot, while Maya insisted it left the tail point. Ownership of the pale tail-base pieces bounced around until Leo took them. Tom's bottom outline stopped short of the branch point. Maya put the pad down once the nose was in.

When time ran out, everyone called it a fish, but only 6 of 143 dominoes fell. The blind judges guessed a dark diagonal line, a crack, or a pen stroke.
