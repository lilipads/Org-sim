# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

The team had a short window to build a fish out of dominoes. Priya grabbed the pad and sketched the outline, tail on the left and nose on the right. Tom asked whether the start was deliberate, and Priya explained it sat at the tail top so every line ran one way. When Tom flagged a fin running backward, Priya agreed: "Drop the backward fin line."

Priya extended the body into the nose, and Leo carried the bottom edge back toward the tail. He chose not to retrace the bottom line already on the pad, since that would lift the top edge already standing. Maya inspected the tail join and called it solid.

Late on, Leo found a jammed domino in the middle of the bottom edge and nudged it back into line. Priya's call was clear: "a short run connected to the start beats a longer one that's loose." Tom built the tail notch, skipping the eye, and Maya checked its join by eye.

At the push, the whole chain fell: 98 of 98. Everyone said fish, though the blind judges guessed sailboats, birds, and leaves.
