# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

Priya suggested a fish, and the team split the pad: Maya took the top line, Tom the bottom, and Leo and Priya the tail. Priya said she had sketched the fish, but the pad was blank, and Maya noted it was already drawn. Priya then fixed a jammed joint at the start.

The tail caused the most trouble. Maya asked Leo to hold off so they wouldn't bump, then Priya found the pad had only the top edge and back of the fin. She ran the missing edge from the junction down, and Leo warned her to stop short so the ends could join.

Near the end, Priya scrapped her planned nudges: "the whole fish already falls through from the start." Hands stayed off the tail. At the push, she wished the little fish luck. The result: 119 of 119 fell. Blind judges guessed a fish with a looped or crossed tail.
