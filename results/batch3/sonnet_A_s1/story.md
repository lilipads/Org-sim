# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

The team chose a fish for a timed domino challenge. Maya sketched the plan: a body outline with a forked tail. Priya laid the top line, Leo the belly and lower fork, and Tom the tail.

Maya noticed the top line running flat past the nose. Priya said it was lifting, and after a closer look Maya agreed: "Ah, you're right Priya." Leo then flagged a sharp turn where Tom's notch began, and Tom lifted it to rebuild with a softer curve.

Leo asked about a stray line below the tail join. Tom said those were his leftovers from the notch attempt, and Maya lifted them. Priya spotted a sideways break at the join and nudged it into line.

Near the end, Tom dropped the notch and kept a plain forked tail. "A clean forked tail reads as a fish," Priya said. Everyone kept their hands off. In the end, 120 of 126 dominoes fell. The team called it a fish, while the blind judges guessed fish, leaf, or bird.
