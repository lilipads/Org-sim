## In short

The team set out to build a fish from dominoes. Hana sketched it on the pad and split the work: Omar took the lead-in and top curve, Sofia the bottom, Jack the tail. Sofia was already partway along her curve, so Hana stopped hers. Omar laid the lower tail diagonal first, so Hana took the back edge instead.

Jack spotted a gap before the lower diagonal. "I'm bridging it now," he said, and Sofia carried on down to meet it. Then Hana pointed out that the back edge had to fall downward, so building up from the bottom would not chain. Jack agreed and carried it all the way down himself.

In the final stretch, Hana found the lower tail was one line, not doubled. Jack nudged a jammed bottom tip into line, and Sofia lifted stray pieces by the tail. Hands off, the team waited for the push. Result: 112 of 115 fell, and every blind judge guessed fish.
