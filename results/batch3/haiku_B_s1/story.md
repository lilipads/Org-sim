# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You love board games and get properly into the rules.
- **Omar**: You played bass in a band for years.
- **Hana**: You keep lists and like to know the plan before you start, and you're usually the first to say out loud when something isn't working.
- **Jack**: You're friendly, chatty and slightly clumsy, you enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and you hand out credit freely.

## In short

Hana opened the table with a plan: sketch a fish, start the run at the tail fin, go along the top to the nose, and come back along the belly. Jack and Sofia agreed. Hana laid the top edge, and Omar traced the tail fins and checked the notch. Jack started the belly at the nose and worked back toward the tail, while Sofia began the bottom at the tail fin and came east to meet him.

The turning point came at the join. The two ends met head-on, and Sofia's bridge didn't take because "there's already a domino too close." She lifted her last bottom dominoes and set out to reconnect a little further back. Jack held still, and Hana and Omar kept their hands off. The join stayed jammed as time ran out, with Sofia still reconnecting.

Result: 99 of 120 fell. Every player called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed fish, bird, boot, and rocket shapes.
