# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

Priya proposed a fish, sketched it on the pad, and split the work: Maya took the nose stroke, Tom the top of the body, Leo the bottom, and Priya the tail. Early on, the lines connected cleanly. "Nose is down and it's all connected," Maya reported, and Tom and Leo set off from the split.

Trouble gathered at the tail. The top tip was too sharp a corner, so Priya bridged it round while Tom cleared the old back edge and filled its middle. The crossing, where body met tail, proved the hardest joint. Priya bridged one side, then Leo cleared the jammed middle and re-laid it while the others kept their hands off. As time ran low, he was still laying it: "Still laying the crossing, nearly there." The team finished with 91 of 140 fallen. Everyone said fish, but the blind judges saw a lure, a balloon, and a knot.
