# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

At this table, a fish took shape one domino at a time. Maya proposed it: "Tail on the left, pointed nose on the right." Priya took the top edge, and Tom took the bottom. Maya sketched the top edge on the pad and laid the body underside from the nose, so the plan was on paper before anyone placed a domino.

The middle stretch was slow. Priya kept announcing "tracing now," and Maya asked Tom to check the pad before he traced. Leo watched the nose join and nudged a jammed domino back into line, while Priya fixed the off-line ones by the tail.

The turning point came near the end. Maya noticed that the lower edge "still stops short just past the tail," and Tom agreed. With little time left, they both worked on that gap. When the start went down, 55 of 55 dominoes fell. The team said fish, and the blind judges guessed fish, eye, or leaf.
