## In short

Sofia proposed a fish, with the forked tail as the giveaway. Hana sketched the body, but Sofia caught a problem: START belonged at the nose, not the tail, so the fins would fall in the right direction. Hana agreed, redrew the pad, and split the work. She took the top edge, Sofia the bottom, Jack waited to add the tail, and Omar watched the fin join.

The top edge reached the tail join but kinked sharply there. Hana's nudges placed nothing, and her inspections kept flagging the same bend. Sofia kept laying the bottom edge, and Jack offered to take her last few. Hana re-traced the top edge from the nose, but the clock kept running.

With time almost gone, Hana wrote, "I'm stopping my hands." Sofia's last dominoes reached the join, yet the kink never closed. The fins stayed on hold. "Nobody touch the join. We push as we are."

Result: 37 of 70 fell. Everyone said fish. The judges saw only a curve, a wavy chain of toppled blocks.
