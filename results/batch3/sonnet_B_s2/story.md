## In short

The team chose a fish, with a big body and a clear tail. Hana sketched it, but Sofia spotted that the start sat at the tail, not the nose. Hana redrew it so the first stroke left the nose.

Jack built his first line on the wrong side and cleared it. Hana took the bottom line and the back half of the belly, and Jack linked his belly into hers. Omar cleared stray dominoes low in the tail.

The start knocked over only one domino, so Sofia took the nose. She found a sharp turn at the corner, not a gap, and laid a curved bridge. When her connect would not cross the gap, Hana laid a backup link from above with two minutes left.

When time ran out, 81 of 233 dominoes fell. Hana's last call was "Top still looks pale to me." Everyone had said fish, but the blind judges guessed a winding river, a squiggle, or a rope.
