# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You coach your daughter's football team and you're in a pub quiz league.
- **Tom**: You took up pottery last year and love making things with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm when things go wrong and you notice when someone's struggling, but with several good options in front of you, you can go back and forth.
- **Leo**: You're a keen birdwatcher and patient with fiddly things.

## In short

At this table, everyone agreed on a fish: side view, with a forked tail. Maya sketched the plan on the pad and laid the top of the body. Leo took the bottom, Priya took the tail fin, and Tom was already running the long bottom line. "Tom, bottom's yours, carry it through," Maya said, and the body began to take shape.

The turning point came at the nose. The top would not join the start chain, and a pale domino stalled the whole build. Leo bridged the gap until Maya told him to stop: "it's not taking." Tom looked from underneath, and Maya joined the nose herself. "Nose is live," she called, and the start finally carried into the top.

Priya re-laid the fin's far end, and Leo fixed a sideways domino at the junction. When time ran out, 116 of 136 fell.

The players saw a fish. The blind judges saw less clearly. Some guessed a fish, but others guessed a dog, a lizard, a horse, and a whale.
