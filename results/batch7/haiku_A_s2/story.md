# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

The team chose a fish. Maya sketched the plan on the pad, Leo laid the spine from the tail toward the nose, and Tom took the forked tail while Priya waited to run the belly line.

The turning point came at a bend. Leo found that the spine wasn't connected through: "There's a gap right at this bend." Everything past it was cut off from the start. He tried a nudge and then a bridge, but an off-side domino kept the chain from hooking.

As time ran out, Tom and Priya were still waiting to hook on. "Bend's broken, everyone," Leo called. "No new work, we're out of time."

Only 6 of 127 fell, stopping at the jammed bend. Everyone at the table said fish. The blind judges saw a curved line, a dark squiggle, and a zigzag crack.
