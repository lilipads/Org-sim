# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Jack sketched a fish with a big tail and split the work. Sofia took the bottom line from the nose, Omar the upper tail line, Hana the lower tail, and Jack the top of the body. "Friendly race to see who finishes first!" he said.

The first turning point came when Hana began tracing the back edge of the tail. Sofia asked her to stop: "Please hold off on that one so we don't double up!" Hana moved to closing the gap where Jack's top line met the tail, and the chain connected from the nose.

The second came near the end. Jack spotted pale dominoes that weren't linked to anything. Hana said they weren't from her build and pulled them, and Sofia cleared the loose ones. The team agreed: "Hands off for the last stretch!"

At the push, the fish fell with a cheer of "Go little fish!" 109 of 111 dominoes went down. Every player saw a fish, but the blind judges guessed leaves, birds, and figure-eights.
