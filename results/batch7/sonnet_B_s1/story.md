# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

At a table, four teammates set out to build a fish no one could mistake. Jack wanted to sketch it himself, but Sofia reached the pad first and drew the fish, and Jack conceded: "Sofia, you win that round!" The work then split cleanly. Jack traced the top line from the nose, Sofia laid the bottom line from the same spot, and Omar and Hana took the tail forks.

Hana warned that the top line had to reach the tail crossing, so everyone aimed for that meeting point. When the lines joined, Sofia confirmed the start was knocking over nearly everything. Hana inspected the tail, judged the notch fine, and called the stray domino behind the nose harmless.

With hands off and the facilitator ready, the team called out "Go fishy go!" The little fish swam, and 126 of 127 dominoes fell. The blind judges named it a fish with a forked tail.
