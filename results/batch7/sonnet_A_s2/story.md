## In short

Priya sketched a fish on the pad and split the work: "Maya, can you take the bottom line? Tom, the tail?" Maya laid the bottom line, and Tom started the tail, but it stalled at the junction, where a jammed domino sat too close to its neighbor. Maya spotted the problem, Leo suggested a retrace, and Tom pulled the piece out and carried the tail on by hand, fanning out from the junction.

The turning point came when Maya offered to take the lower arm. Tom gave her the go-ahead: "Branch it off the junction and run it down and out toward the bottom of the tail." Maya ran it to the bottom corner, and Leo brought the back edge down to meet her with a soft curve. Priya checked the whole table and found everything connected, tail included. Then Tom said, "Hands off, everyone," and nobody touched it again.

When the push came, all 121 dominoes fell, and the blind judges saw a fish.
