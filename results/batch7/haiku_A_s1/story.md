# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Maya**: You're relaxed and easy to work with, happy to go along with a good plan.
- **Tom**: You're warm and funny once you're comfortable, and you love working with your hands.
- **Priya**: You stay calm under pressure and notice when someone needs help.
- **Leo**: You're patient and precise with fiddly work.

## In short

At a twenty-minute domino table, the team set out to build a fish. Maya drew the top edge from the nose, Leo laid the bottom edge, Tom took the fin, and Priya built the tail lobes. The lobes were finished early but waited at the fork for the body to arrive. As Tom put it, "they'll pick up once the body gets there."

Trouble started at the nose. Priya's first bridge was "a dead end," and the top edge stayed pale there. She added a short connect, then nudged the joint, but "the nose connect didn't carry into the top edge; it's still cut off there." Leo pointed to the sharp turn at the fork as the likely weak spot. Maya was still short of the fork when the timer ran out.

When the start domino fell, 44 of 141 dominoes went down. Every player called it a fish, but the blind judges guessed a hook or a bird's head.
