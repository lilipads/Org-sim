## In short

Maya sketched the fish on the pad, starting at the nose and running the top line back. Priya took the tail, and Leo stayed off it so they wouldn't double up. Then the tail sat empty until Tom noticed. "I don't see the tail on the table yet," he said, and laid it himself. Priya agreed: "The tail's yours." Once the top line reached the fork, Tom found a domino that didn't fit, and Priya closed the gap where the belly met the tail. With little time left, Maya walked the run from the nose, and everyone kept their hands off the start. When the run went, 126 of 132 fell. Blind judges saw a fish, though a few guessed a leaf or a bird.
