*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

## In short

Sofia grabbed the pad and sketched a fish, side view, tail on the left. Omar spoke up: start at the nose, not the tail, because both body lines leave the nose heading the same way. Sofia agreed, and the team followed the nose start.

Then Hana found the trouble. The start domino sat short of the nose, and the nose dominoes weren't connected. She moved the start and bridged the gap, but her "it's dark" calls kept proving wrong. "Honest update: the nose still isn't connected," she admitted more than once.

With minutes slipping away, Sofia made the call: "let's stop waiting." Omar laid the top edge and Jack the belly straight off the start, while Sofia took the lower fin.

When time ran out, only 8 of 134 dominoes fell. Everyone said fish. Blind judges guessed a curved row, a bracket, an arc.
