# AI pilot (real-time) · table of 4

*4 people, 10 minutes, one brief: make a picture of an animal out of dominoes.*

**The table**

- **Sofia**: You're upbeat and full of ideas, quick to suggest a way forward and quick to get started.
- **Omar**: You're easy-going and good-humoured, with a strong eye for how things look.
- **Hana**: You're organised and like a clear plan before you start.
- **Jack**: You're friendly and talkative, enjoy a bit of friendly competition, and are generous with credit when things go well.

## In short

Everyone agreed on a fish within minutes. Jack proposed the outline, and Sofia took the spine. Then Omar suggested starting at the tail, so the fall would sweep the whole body. "Good call, Omar," Sofia replied, and the plan turned around.

Roles split quickly. Hana took the tail fin and changed it so its arms fanned forward and met the body just behind the start. Jack finished the top line, Sofia kept the spine and bottom line, and Omar built the top fin.

The turning point came late. Omar warned that the start was "only carrying" a few pieces, and Hana confirmed the spine was pale just past the tail fin. Sofia moved to close the break at the branch, a bump knocked a batch over, and Omar ran the last stretch, which ended just short of the nose.

When time ran out, 9 of 63 dominoes fell. Every player called it a fish, but the judges guessed a snake, a squiggle, or a winding river.
